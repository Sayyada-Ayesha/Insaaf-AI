# INSAaf AI — Document & Drafting Taxonomy (Spec Parts 14-16)

Corpus: 2.4.0-pre-freeze (post 2026-09-06 expansion) — generated 2026-09-06

Purpose: map every Pakistan document/drafting type the corpus can support for final_v3 document_explanation / document_drafting training rows, with honest FULL / PARTIAL / SUMMARY / GAP status. No drafting row may exceed the stated status.

Status scale: **FULL** = required content/steps verified in indexed corpus or official pages (canonical for drafting rows); **PARTIAL** = only part of the drafting rules indexed; drafting rows must teach caveats, never invent omitted rules; **SUMMARY** = digest-level summaries only (topic areas, not section text); **GAP** = governing law not indexed; no drafting rows generated for it.

## Consequence for final_v3

- document_explanation rows may draw on any type above (explaining what a document is/does) with the same status ceiling.
- document_drafting rows are generated ONLY for FULL/PARTIAL/SUMMARY types and must (a) stay within the corpus-grounded facts and flow, and (b) for PARTIAL/SUMMARY explicitly caveat what must be verified (forms, fees, prescribed formats, registration-office steps).
- GAP types (power_of_attorney) appear in refusal/scope rows: the assistant explains the document exists and directs to a lawyer/registration office without drafting.
- Prescribed-form texts (CPC Orders, court forms, NADRA/DGIP/ECP forms) remain external; the corpus records the verified process around them, not the form templates.
