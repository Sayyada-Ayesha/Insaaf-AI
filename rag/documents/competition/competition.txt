[SECTION: 1] Competition Act, 2010 (Act No. XIX of 2010) — Promotes competition and prevents anti-competitive practices in Pakistan. Source: CCP (cc.gov.pk). Status: CURRENT.

[SECTION: 2] Competition Commission of Pakistan (CCP) — CCP is the regulatory authority. Functions: (a) prevent anti-competitive agreements; (b) prevent abuse of dominant position; (c) regulate mergers and acquisitions; (d) promote competition advocacy.

[SECTION: 3] Prohibited agreements — Any agreement between undertakings that prevents, restricts or distorts competition is prohibited. Includes: price fixing, market sharing, bid rigging, output restriction, collective boycott. Penalty: up to PKR 75 million or 10% of annual turnover.

[SECTION: 4] Abuse of dominant position — An undertaking with dominant position may not: (a) impose unfair prices or trading conditions; (b) limit production or technical development; (c) apply dissimilar conditions to equivalent transactions; (d) make contracts subject to supplementary obligations unrelated to the contract.

[SECTION: 5] Mergers and acquisitions — Mergers, acquisitions or takeovers that substantially lessen competition must be notified to CCP. CCP may approve, approve with conditions, or prohibit the combination.

[SECTION: 6] Leniency program — A party to a cartel may apply for leniency (reduction/immunity from penalty) in exchange for full cooperation and disclosure of cartel activity. First applicant may receive full immunity.

[SECTION: 7] Appeals — CCP orders may be appealed to the Competition Appellate Tribunal within 30 days. Further appeal to the Supreme Court within 60 days.
