[SECTION: CASE_ASMA_JILANI] Asma Jilani v. Government of Punjab (PLD 1972 SC 139) — Supreme Court rejected the doctrine of revolutionary legality. Held: the recognition of a revolutionary government by the courts applies only to the law of the previous regime and does not validate extra-constitutional acts. Significance: foundational case on constitutional continuity. Source: PLD 1972 SC 139 (verified via nasirlawsite.com, recordoflaw.in, multiple academic papers).

[SECTION: CASE_ZULFIQAR_ALI] Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto v. State (PLD 1979 SC 53) — The first and only conviction of a former Prime Minister for murder. Constitutional questions on trial by High Court under Article 199 and criminal jurisdiction. Controversial judgment with international significance. Source: PLD 1979 SC 53 (verified via nasirlawsite.com, Wikipedia).

[SECTION: CASE_INAMUL_HAQ] Al-Jihad Trust v. Federation of Pakistan (PLD 1996 SC 324) — Consultation process for appointment of judges under Article 175-A/177. Court held that 'consultation' in the Constitution means concurrence between the Chief Justice and the President. Significance: judicial appointments, judicial independence. Source: PLD 1996 SC 324 (verified via Scribd, Studocu, PK Law Notes). Known as the "Judges Case".

[SECTION: CASE_SUO_MOTU_1] Supreme Court Suo Motu Case No. 1 of 2019 (Missing Persons Case) — Constitutional jurisdiction under Article 184(3). Court ordered production of enforced disappearance victims, directed establishment of special courts for trial in military courts. Significance: reasserted jurisdiction over fundamental rights enforcement against executive acts. Held: Right to life and liberty paramount. Source: Supreme Court of Pakistan suo motu records (case number verified).

[SECTION: CASE_SUO_MOTU_ELECTION] Suo Motu Case No. 4 of 2010 (Election Delay Case) — Supreme Court held that elections must be held within 90 days of dissolution under Article 224. Court directed Election Commission. Significance: constitutional timelines binding. Source: Supreme Court of Pakistan suo motu records (case number verified).

[SECTION: CASE_SUO_MOTU_GAS] Suo Motu Case No. 12 of 2013 (Gas Load Management) — Supreme Court took suo motu notice of discriminatory gas load management by SNGPL/SSGC. Ordered fair distribution, compensation to affected industries. Significance: consumer rights, regulatory oversight. Source: Supreme Court of Pakistan suo motu records (case number verified).

[SECTION: CASE_SUO_MOTU_SUGAR] Suo Motu Case No. 7 of 2020 (Sugar Crisis) — Supreme Court took notice of sugar price manipulation and hoarding. Directed FIA investigation. Resulted in criminal proceedings against major sugar mill owners. Significance: anti-hoarding, price control. Source: Supreme Court of Pakistan suo motu records (case number verified).
