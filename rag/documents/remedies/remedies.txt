[SECTION: REMEDY_POLICE_FIR_REFUSAL]
Title: Situation to Action: Police Refusing to Register FIR
Authority: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (All Provinces)
Legal Status: Actionable Legal Guidance
Source URL: https://pakistancode.gov.pk

User Situation: "Police meri FIR register nahi kar rahi." / Police refusing to record crime report.
Legal Analysis: Registration of FIR for cognizable offences (theft, robbery, assault, murder, fraud) is legally mandatory under Section 154 CrPC.
Actionable Steps:
1. Obtain copy of written complaint submitted to SHO.
2. Send written complaint via registered post to SP / SSP / DPO Police.
3. If unresolved in 7 days, file application under Section 22A(6) CrPC in District Sessions Court.
4. Court will order SHO to register FIR. Alternatively, file direct private complaint under Section 200 CrPC before Judicial Magistrate.

[SECTION: REMEDY_CYBER_BLACKMAIL]
Title: Situation to Action: Online Blackmail and Cyber Harassment
Authority: Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA), 2016
Jurisdiction: Pakistan
Legal Status: Actionable Legal Guidance
Source URL: https://complaint.fia.gov.pk

User Situation: "Someone is blackmailing me online." / "Kisi ne meri pictures hack karke blackmail shuru kar diya."
Legal Analysis: Cyber harassment, extortion, unauthorized data copying, and modesty violation are criminal offences under PECA 2016 (Sections 13, 16, 19, 24).
Actionable Steps:
1. DO NOT delete evidence. Take clean screenshots of profiles, messages, numbers, and URL links.
2. Do not yield to blackmail demands.
3. Submit online complaint at FIA Cybercrime Wing portal: https://complaint.fia.gov.pk or call 1991 helpline.
4. Visit nearest FIA Cybercrime Reporting Centre (CCRC) for statement recording.

[SECTION: REMEDY_UNLAWFUL_EMPLOYER_TERMINATION]
Title: Situation to Action: Termination Without Notice or Wages Withheld
Authority: Industrial & Commercial Employment (Standing Orders) Ordinance, 1968
Jurisdiction: Pakistan
Legal Status: Actionable Legal Guidance
Source URL: https://pakistancode.gov.pk

User Situation: "My employer fired me without notice and is not paying my salary." / "Malik ne baghair kisi notice ke naukri se nikal diya."
Legal Analysis: Permanent employees cannot be terminated without written notice (or 1 month salary in lieu) and due process under Standing Order 12.
Actionable Steps:
1. Send written grievance notice to employer within 60 days of termination.
2. If employer fails to settle dues within 15 days, file Grievance Petition under Industrial Relations Act before Labour Court.
3. Labour Court can order full reinstatement and back-wages.

[SECTION: REMEDY_DEFECTIVE_PRODUCT_CONSUMER]
Title: Situation to Action: Sold Defective Product / Refusing Refund
Authority: Provincial Consumer Protection Acts (Punjab, Sindh, KP, ICT)
Jurisdiction: Pakistan
Legal Status: Actionable Legal Guidance
Source URL: https://punjabcode.punjab.gov.pk

User Situation: "I bought a defective product and the shopkeeper is refusing refund or warranty."
Legal Analysis: Selling defective goods or providing substandard services violates Consumer Protection Acts.
Actionable Steps:
1. Send 15-day written legal notice to shopkeeper/manufacturer via registered post.
2. If unresolved, file claim in District Consumer Court within 30 days of cause of action.
3. NO court fee required for consumer court complaints. Court can order full refund, product replacement, and financial compensation for mental agony.
