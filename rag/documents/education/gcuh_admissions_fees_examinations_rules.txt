Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Sindh — GCU Hyderabad, institution-specific)
Source: GCUH Undergraduate Admission Policy 2026-27

Reserved quota categories named in the policy: "Sports Quota, Disability Quota, GCUH Employees Quota, and Religious Minorities Quota".
"any candidate wishing to apply under any reserved quota category ... shall be required to submit a separate physical application at the Admissions Desk, GC University Hyderabad (GCUH) along with the required supporting documents/proof for the respective quota category, such as a Disability Certificate, Sports Certificate, Employee Proof, or other relevant documents, as applicable. Sports quota seats "will be filled on the basis of trials conducted for the candidates".
"In case any seats remain vacant under any reserved quota, such seats shall be transferred" to the general pool per the policy's own clause.
Practical point for a student: applying online is not sufficient for a quota seat — the separate physical application with quota proof at the Admissions Desk is required by the same policy document.

Source: GCUH Undergraduate Admission Policy 2026-27

[SECTION: GCUH_ADMISSION_CANCELLATION_AND_FEE_REFUND_ROUTE]
Title: GCU Hyderabad admission cancellation application route and HEC Fee Refund Policy linkage
Authority: Government College University Hyderabad (GCUH); refund quantified by the latest HEC Fee Refund Policy
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Sindh — GCU Hyderabad, institution-specific)
Source: GCUH Undergraduate Admission Policy 2026-27, clauses (xvi) and General Information item 6
Source URL: https://gcuh.edu.pk/pages/undergraduate_policy.html

"For cancellation of admission, the candidate/student shall submit an application to the Director Admissions through the concerned Head of Department and Dean of the Faculty for further processing."
"A candidate who secures admission by paying the prescribed fee against a seat and subsequently applies for cancellation of provisional admission shall be eligible for a fee refund in accordance with the latest HEC Fee Refund Policy."
Different situation, different result — the same policy states: "An applicant who is already registered or admitted in any degree program at GC University Hyderabad shall be required to cancel their existing admission on or before the closing date of admissions for the current session. Failure to do so may result in the cancellation of the new application/admission, and any fee deposited shall not be refundable."
So at GCUH: cancellation of a provisional admission after paying against a seat is refund-eligible under the HEC policy, while the fee on a new application cancelled for duplicate registration is expressly non-refundable. The route is always a written application through the Head of Department and the Dean of the Faculty to the Director Admissions — not a bank claim.
The percentage slabs of the HEC Fee Refund Policy itself are recorded in the HEC corpus records, not here.
Text read from the official policy page on 2026-10-16.
