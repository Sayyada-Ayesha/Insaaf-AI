"Admissions should be advertised in the prominent newspapers of national and regional repute as well as on the University website gcuh.edu.pk and social media."
"The candidates who intend to apply for admission to bachelor's degree programs for the academic year 2026-27 may fill in online registration / admission form at (https://adm.gcuh.edu.pk) by following given guidelines at GCUH website."
"A valid CNIC, email address, mobile number with WhatsApp number are mandatory to complete the registration process."
"Applicants are required to deposit Rs. 3,000/- (non-refundable) as registration/processing fee through a challan generated via the prescribed Allied Bank Limited Account No. 0010158949730040".
"Payment may be made at any ABL branch across Pakistan or through the 1Bill option available in Mobile Banking Apps, Mobile Wallets, ATMs, etc. No other modes of payment (such as Fund Transfer, Credit Card, etc.) shall be accepted."
"The candidate must upload a passport size picture with a white or blue background, original color scanned copies (100dpi) of all the required documents, paid bank challan or receipt of mobile banking application as indicated above."
"The eligibility of the candidate for admission shall be decided by the University after scrutinizing the documents provided by the candidates."
"Since the registration/admission form is legal document, any wrong information provided therein or tempering it in any other way is illegal and may result in rejection of the form out rightly."
Scope: these are GCU Hyderabad's own rules for its own 2026-27 undergraduate cycle. They are not the rules of any other Sindh or Pakistani university, and they are not nationwide HEC requirements unless the document says so.

Once a candidate confirms admission in a department/program, the confirmation shall be treated as final, and no change of department/program or transfer to another department/program shall be allowed at any later stage of the admission process."
