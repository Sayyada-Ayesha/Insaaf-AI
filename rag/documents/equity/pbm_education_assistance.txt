Title: Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal Individual Financial Assistance (Education) — eligibility and eligible institutions
Authority: Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal (PBM), Ministry of State for Poverty Alleviation and Rural Development
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Federal)
Source: Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal — Project IFA page
Source URL: https://www.pbm.gov.pk/Project-IFA.html
Retrieval Method: Official page text read on 2026-10-14

PBM provides education assistance to deserving poor students studying in Government schools, colleges, universities, or technical institutes. Self-financed/private students and children of Govt. servants are not eligible.
Through Individual Financial Assistance (Education), PBM supports poor, deserving, and brilliant students enrolled in public-sector educational and technical institutions with education stipends for completing their studies.
Assistance is provided only to regular/merit-based students in government institutes. Self-financed or private institute students, and children of government employees, are ineligible.

Title: Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal Individual Financial Assistance — other programme sections and what they do not govern
Authority: Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal (PBM), Ministry of State for Poverty Alleviation and Rural Development
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Federal)
Source: Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal — Project IFA page
Source URL: https://www.pbm.gov.pk/Project-IFA.html
Retrieval Method: Official page text read on 2026-10-14

IFA General (Financial Assistance): Individuals having monthly income up to Rs. 37,000/- are eligible. Categories listed include poor unable to overcome financial misery, victims of sudden fall in economic status, severe disability preventing livelihood, widowed/divorced/separated women raising children, and infirm persons without family support.
Special Friends (Disabled Persons): Families with two or more disabled members are recognized as "Special Friends" and supported with financial assistance — Rs. 20,000/- for families with one disabled person and Rs. 30,000/- for families with two or more disabled persons. Areas of support listed include financial assistance, standard wheel chairs, customized wheel chairs, artificial limbs and hearing aids.
Combination rule as published: assistance under categories such as medical treatment, general financial assistance, education stipend and rehabilitation may be granted within one year, except General assistance and Rehabilitation cannot be combined. Priority is given to widows, disabled and infirm individuals.
Scope limit — read this before quoting a figure: the monthly income ceiling of Rs. 37,000/- and the Rs. 20,000/- and Rs. 30,000/- amounts are published under IFA General and the Special Friends section. The published IFA Education section does NOT state an income ceiling, a stipend amount or a payment schedule. Do not apply the General-assistance income threshold or the disability assistance amounts to education assistance. This page publishes no application opening or closing date, so it does not establish that any intake is currently open, and it states no district-wise quota.
