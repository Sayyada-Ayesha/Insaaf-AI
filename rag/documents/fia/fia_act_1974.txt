# Federal Investigation Agency — FIA Act, 1974 and Jurisdiction
# Coverage: establishment, jurisdiction over scheduled federal offences, investigation powers,
# complaint procedure and oversight. Sources: FIA Act, 1974 (Act VIII-A of 1975) as amended;
# official FIA complaint channels.

[SECTION: FIA_READING_NOTE]
The Federal Investigation Agency is constituted under the Federal Investigation Agency Act, 1974.
FIA investigates offences under specified federal laws — it does NOT have general criminal
jurisdiction over provincial offences (ordinary theft, hurt etc. remain with the police). Before
advising anyone to approach FIA, check that the matter falls in the FIA Schedule (offences under
the listed federal statutes).

[SECTION: FIA_ESTABLISHMENT]
LAW: Federal Investigation Agency Act, 1974. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY PROVISIONS (summary of the operative framework):
- The Agency is constituted by the Federal Government; it has zonal offices (e.g., provincial
  capitals and major cities) headed by zonal directors under a Director General.
- Members of the Agency exercise investigation powers under the Act read with the applicable
  procedural law (Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898, as adapted) for scheduled offences.
- The Federal Government may, by notification, extend FIA's jurisdiction to further offences.
SOURCE: FIA Act, 1974. VERIFICATION: verified as to framework.

[SECTION: FIA_JURISDICTION]
LAW: FIA Act, 1974, Schedule and notifications. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
SCHEDULED JURISDICTION (well-established areas):
- Immigration offences under the Emigration Ordinance, 1979 and the Prevention of Trafficking in
  Persons Act, 2018 (human trafficking, migrant smuggling).
- Passport offences under the Passport Act, 1974.
- Cybercrime offences under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (FIA Cybercrime Wing,
  NR3C); online financial fraud, hacking, electronic forgery, cyberstalking.
- Anti-corruption offences under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947 and the Pakistan Penal
  Code where notified for federal matters.
- Offences against federal property/federal subjects notified by the Federal Government.
- Money laundering investigation support under the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010 framework
  (with other agencies).
NOTE: provincial corruption and ordinary offences are NOT FIA jurisdiction unless notified.
SOURCE: FIA Act, 1974 Schedule. VERIFICATION: verified as to the listed areas (the Schedule is
well-established; individual notification changes need the latest gazette).

[SECTION: FIA_COMPLAINT_PROCEDURE]
PROCEDURE: FIA complaint channels. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
STEPS (summary of the standard route):
- WHO: any person aggrieved by a scheduled offence (or knowing of one) may complain.
- WHERE: FIA zonal office having territorial jurisdiction, or the FIA online complaint portal /
  helpline (official FIA website) for cybercrime and other scheduled matters.
- DOCUMENTS: identity, written complaint describing the offence with evidence (documents,
  transaction records, screenshots, CNIC copy), and, for cybercrime, device/URL/account details.
- STEPS: complaint registered -> preliminary verification -> inquiry -> FIR (case registered) if
  a scheduled offence is disclosed -> investigation -> challan/report.
- ESCALATION: where the zonal office does not proceed, complaint to the zonal director or the
  Director General; courts may also direct investigation in appropriate cases.
SOURCE: official FIA complaint channels and FIA Act. VERIFICATION: verified as to the route
(exact portal details should be checked on the official FIA website).

[SECTION: FIA_NR3C_CYBER]
WING: FIA Cybercrime Wing (NR3C). COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
SUBSTANCE: NR3C receives and investigates complaints of offences under PECA 2016 — hacking,
unauthorized access, electronic fraud (online scams, banking fraud via electronic means),
cyberstalking/harassment, defamation through information systems, identity theft. Complaint
filing: online complaint form with evidence; the complainant should preserve electronic evidence
(URLs, screenshots, account details, transaction IDs).
SOURCE: PECA 2016; FIA NR3C procedures. VERIFICATION: verified as to the framework.

[SECTION: FIA_IMMIGRATION_ANTI_TRAFFICKING]
WING: FIA Anti-Human Trafficking. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
SUBSTANCE: investigates offences under the Prevention of Trafficking in Persons Act, 2018 and the
Emigration Ordinance, 1979 — human trafficking, migrant smuggling, illegal recruitment of
workers for overseas employment, forged travel documents; complaints can be made at FIA
anti-human-trafficking circles/zones.
SOURCE: PACHTO 2018; Emigration Ordinance 1979. VERIFICATION: verified as to the framework.

[SECTION: FIA_LIMITS]
LIMITS: FIA powers are investigation powers, not adjudication; trials take place in courts.
FIA cannot register provincial offences; do not advise FIA complaints for ordinary police
matters. FIA jurisdictional notifications change — verify the current Schedule.
