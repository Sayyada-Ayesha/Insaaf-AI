[SECTION: 1] Provincial Local Government Laws — Each province has its own local government system. Source: Provincial Assemblies. Status: CURRENT (provincial variations apply).

[SECTION: 2] Punjab Local Government Act, 2022 — Three-tier system: (a) District Council (at district level); (b) Municipal Committee/Metropolitan Corporation (at urban/town level); (c) Village Council/Neighbourhood Council (at grassroots level). Chairpersons elected directly. Term: 4 years.

[SECTION: 3] Sindh Local Government Act, 2013 — System includes: Metropolitan Corporation (Karachi/Hyderabad), Municipal Corporations, Municipal Committees, Town Committees, District Councils, and Union Councils. Mayor elected directly.

[SECTION: 4] Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Local Government Act, 2013 — Tehsil-based system with Village Councils and Neighbourhood Councils at grassroots. District Council at district level.

[SECTION: 5] Balochistan Local Government Act — District Council, Municipal Corporations, Municipal Committees, Union Councils. Provincial Local Government Commission oversees.

[SECTION: 6] Functions of local governments — (a) Water supply and sanitation; (b) roads and streets; (c) street lighting; (d) parks and playgrounds; (e) solid waste management; (f) health and education (primary); (g) local tax collection; (h) building regulation; (i) licensing of trades.

[SECTION: 7] Finance — Local governments collect property tax, profession tax, trade license fees, and development charges. Provincial Finance Commission awards funds to local governments. Own-source revenue generation is encouraged.

[SECTION: 8] Islamabad Capital Territory Local Government Act, 2015 — ICT Metropolitan Corporation covers Islamabad. Union Councils are the basic unit. Mayor elected directly.
