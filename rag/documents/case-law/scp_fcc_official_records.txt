[SECTION: CASE_SCP_2026_02]
Court: Supreme Court of Pakistan. Subject: Seniority / Challenge to seniority list. Case No: C.P.L.A.5091/2025. Title: Sahibzada Hassan Ali and others v. The Govt. of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa through Chief Secretary, Peshawar and others. Author Judge: Mr. Justice Muhammad Ali Mazhar. Judgment Date: 19-05-2026. Upload Date: 27-08-2026. Points (as published by the court): (1) delayed publication of notification of regularization of service under KPK Employees Regularization of Service Act, 2018 and its effect under Section 20A of the General Clauses Act, 1897 when employees were regularized from the effective date of the 2018 Act by fiction of law; (2) public authorities should act fairly; (3) employees cannot be made a victim due to delay in publication; (4) the department was responsible to complete the paperwork timely as per law and rules.

[SECTION: CASE_SCP_2026_03]
Court: Supreme Court of Pakistan. Subject: Competition Commission of Pakistan / Monopolistic Practices and Abuse of Dominant Position. Case No: C.A.367/2025. Title: Pakistan Vanaspati Manufacturers Association (PVMA) v. Competition Commission of Pakistan. Author Judge: Mr. Justice Jamal Khan Mandokhail. Judgment Date: 25-08-2026. Upload Date: 25-08-2026. Points (as published by the court): the Competition Act, 2010 mandates that the Commission shall be administratively and functionally independent; the Commission alone is competent to advise undertakings individually to pass on to consumers the benefit of reduction in the price of raw materials, enabling each undertaking to independently determine the prices of its finished products in accordance with its own commercial considerations.
