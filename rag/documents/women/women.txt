[SECTION: 1] Women Protection Laws of Pakistan — Consolidated coverage of key women's legal protections. Sources: Pakistan Code, Ministry of Human Rights, Provincial Assemblies. Status: VARIOUS (federal + provincial).

[SECTION: 2] Protection against Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2010 — Applicable to all workplaces, public and private. Every organization must constitute an Inquiry Committee (3 members, majority women, chairperson must be a woman). Complaint procedure: (a) written complaint to Inquiry Committee; (b) inquiry completed within 30 days; (c) extension of 30 days if needed. Punishment: removal from service, fine, or both. Appeals to Ombudsperson.

[SECTION: 3] Criminal Law Amendment (Protection of Women) Act, 2006 — Amended the Hudood Ordinances. Separated zina (adultery/fornication) from PPC provisions. Rape now dealt under PPC Sections 375-376 (not under Hudood). Burden of proof remains with prosecution. False accusation of zina now an offence.

[SECTION: 4] Acid Control and Acid Crime Prevention Act, 2010 — Defines acid throwing as a specific offence under PPC Section 336-B. Punishment: imprisonment for life or 14 years minimum, and fine of PKR 1 million. The court may also order compensation to the victim. Medical treatment must be provided by the state.

[SECTION: 5] Women in Distress and Detention Fund Act, 2011 — Establishes a fund under the Ministry of Human Rights to provide financial assistance, legal aid, medical care, shelter and rehabilitation to women in distress or detention.

[SECTION: 6] Domestic Violence — Provincial legislation: (a) Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act 2016: protection orders, residence orders, monetary relief, prohibition of violence. (b) Sindh Domestic Violence Act 2013: similar protections. (c) KPK Domestic Violence Act 2021: protection and prevention. Complaints may be made to Protection Officers. Courts may issue interim protection orders.

[SECTION: 7] Women's Right to Inheritance — Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act 1962 confirms women's right to inheritance. The PPC (Section 498-A) criminalizes depriving a woman of inheritance. Women entitled to specific shares under Islamic inheritance law (Quranic heirs).

[SECTION: 8] Dowry and Bridal Gifts — Dowry and bridal gifts (Jahez) are the property of the wife. She may recover them through civil suit or family court. Demand for dowry as a consideration for marriage is prohibited.

[SECTION: 9] Nikkah Registration — Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 5: Every marriage solemnized under Muslim law must be registered with the Nikah Registrar. Failure to register is an offence. The Nikah Nama (marriage contract) is a legally binding document.

[SECTION: 10] Women's Right to Maintenance — Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 9: If a husband fails to maintain his wife, the wife may apply to the Family Court which may order maintenance at a rate deemed reasonable.

[SECTION: 11] Protection of Women against Violence — Provincial Domestic Violence laws provide: (a) protection orders preventing the accused from committing violence; (b) residence orders giving the victim exclusive use of shared residence; (c) monetary relief for expenses; (d) custody of minor children to the victim.

[SECTION: 12] Workplace Harassment — The Harassment Act 2010 applies to all employees, interns, patients, students and visitors. Sexual harassment defined broadly including unwelcome advances, requests for sexual favors, verbal/non-verbal conduct. Employers must display the Code of Conduct. Complaint mechanism includes Ombudsperson at provincial level.

[SECTION: 13] Women Prisoners — Special provisions: (a) women prisoners must be kept separate from men; (b) female guards must supervise women prisoners; (c) infants may stay with mothers in prison; (d) pregnant women entitled to special nutrition and medical care.

[SECTION: 14] Gender-Based Violence Shelters — Provincial governments maintain shelter homes for women in distress. These include: Dar-ul-Amans (Punjab), Women Crisis Centers (Sindh), Shelter Homes (KPK, Balochistan). Services: safe accommodation, legal aid, medical care, counseling, vocational training.

[SECTION: 15] Helplines — National Helpline for Women: 1043 (Ministry of Human Rights). Provincial helplines: Punjab Women Helpline 1043, Sindh Women Helpline 1080. Police Women Complaint Cells at every police station. Madadgar 15: emergency police assistance.
