# Power of Attorney in Pakistan — Law, Execution, Registration and Drafting
# Coverage: Powers of Attorney Act 1882; Registration Act 1908 registration requirements;
# stamping; execution and attestation (domestic and overseas); revocation; drafting
# structures in English and Urdu Script. Sources: Powers of Attorney Act, 1882; Registration
# Act, 1908; Stamp Act, 1899; settled practice for notarization/consular attestation.

[SECTION: POA_READING_NOTE]
A power of attorney (mukhtar-nama) is a written instrument by which one person (the
principal/donor) authorizes another (the attorney/agent) to act on his behalf. Pakistani
law governing POAs: the Powers of Attorney Act, 1882 (definition and general effect), the
Registration Act, 1908 (when registration is compulsory), and the Stamp Act, 1899 (stamp
duty). Requirements below state the settled position; where a figure (stamp duty, fee) is
not verified it is marked NOT VERIFIED.

[SECTION: POA_TYPES]
TYPES (Powers of Attorney Act, 1882 framework):
- General POA: authorizes the attorney to do all acts the principal could do, or a wide
  class of acts (property dealings, litigation, banking, representation before offices).
- Special POA: confined to a specific act or matter (e.g., to execute one sale deed, to
  represent in one case, to collect one specific payment).
- Property POA: authorizes dealings with immovable property (sale, purchase, mortgage,
  mutation). Registration considerations apply (see POA_REGISTRATION).
- Overseas POA: executed by a Pakistani abroad for use in Pakistan, or executed in Pakistan
  for use abroad — requires notarization and consular attestation (see POA_OVERSEAS).
SOURCE: Powers of Attorney Act, 1882, Section 2 definition framework. VERIFICATION: verified.

[SECTION: POA_REGISTRATION]
LAW: Registration Act, 1908, Sections 17 and 18; settled case law. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY POSITION (summary):
- A POA that CREATES or ASSIGNS an interest in immovable property, or that empowers the
  attorney to sell, mortgage or otherwise transfer immovable property, requires compulsory
  registration under Section 17 (read with Section 49: an unregistered instrument of that
  class cannot affect immovable property and is inadmissible as evidence of the transaction).
- A POA for general representation (appearing in offices, litigation, filing documents) or
  for acts not transferring immovable property does not require compulsory registration.
- A power coupled with an interest (where the attorney has an interest in the subject
  matter) is irrevocable during that interest.
SOURCE: Registration Act, 1908, ss.17/49; settled case law. VERIFICATION: verified as to the
settled position (the sale/mortgage POA registration requirement is well-established).

[SECTION: POA_STAMPING]
LAW: Stamp Act, 1899 (Article 48 POA schedule, as amended provincially). COVERAGE: SUMMARY.
KEY POSITION: a POA must be stamped with the prescribed stamp duty (general vs special POA
rates differ; provincial amendments vary). Understamped documents cannot be received in
evidence (subject to the penalty/validation procedure). AMOUNT: NOT VERIFIED — check the
current provincial stamp schedule.
SOURCE: Stamp Act, 1899. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: POA_EXECUTION]
EXECUTION AND ATTESTATION (settled practice):
- The principal signs (or thumb-impresses if illiterate) in the presence of witnesses.
- Two witnesses attest the principal's signature/thumb impression; for an illiterate
  principal the thumb impression must be attested with the attestation recording that the
  contents were read over and understood.
- Attestation before a Notary Public (notarized POA) is standard for use in offices, banks
  and abroad.
- For court/pleading POAs (vakalatnama for lawyers) the court rules apply.
SOURCE: settled notarial and stamping practice. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: POA_OVERSEAS]
OVERSEAS POA (Pakistani abroad or foreign POA for use in Pakistan):
- Execute before a Notary Public in the country of execution (as per that country's law —
  home-country step).
- Then attestation: the Pakistani Embassy/Consulate in that country attests the notarized
  POA (consular attestation) for use in Pakistan; where the foreign country is a party to
  the Apostille Convention and Pakistan accepts the channel, an apostille may apply —
  verify the current channel with the Pakistani mission.
- After attestation, the POA is submitted/registered in Pakistan where registration is
  required (property POAs).
- Consular use abroad of a POA executed in Pakistan: notarize in Pakistan, then attest from
  the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the foreign mission in Pakistan as required by the
  destination country (destination-country requirement).
SOURCE: MOFA consular attestation practice. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: POA_REVOCATION]
REVOCATION (Powers of Attorney Act, 1882, Section 201-208 framework):
- The principal may revoke the POA at any time (unless it is coupled with an interest).
- Revocation by: a revocation deed (registered where the POA was registered), or written
  notice to the attorney, and public notice to persons dealing with the attorney.
- Termination: POA also terminates on the death, insolvency or incapacity of the principal
  (subject to the law), or on completion of the specified act (special POA).
- Until notice of revocation reaches them, third parties who deal with the attorney in good
  faith may be protected.
SOURCE: Powers of Attorney Act, 1882. VERIFICATION: verified as to the framework.

[SECTION: POA_DRAFTING_STRUCTURE_EN]
DRAFTING STRUCTURE — GENERAL POA (English formal document skeleton):
1. Heading: "GENERAL POWER OF ATTORNEY"
2. Preamble: donor identification — "I, [NAME], son/daughter of [PARENT], holder of CNIC
 [CNIC NUMBER], resident of [ADDRESS], do hereby appoint ..."
3. Attorney identification: "Mr./Ms. [NAME], son/daughter of [PARENT], holder of CNIC
 [CNIC NUMBER], resident of [ADDRESS], as my true and lawful attorney."
4. Scope clause: "to do and execute all or any of the following acts, deeds and things on
 my behalf: (a) to manage, sell, mortgage, lease or otherwise deal with my movable and
 immovable property; (b) to appear, act and represent me before all courts, offices,
 banks and authorities; (c) to sign, execute and register all deeds, documents and
 applications; (d) to receive and recover monies due to me and to give valid receipts;
 (e) to do all other acts incidental or necessary to the above."
5. Ratification clause: "I hereby ratify and confirm all acts lawfully done by my attorney
 in pursuance of this power."
6. Date and place; 7. Signature of the donor; 8. Attestation of two witnesses (name,
 CNIC, signature); 9. Notarial attestation where required.
NOTE: for a SPECIAL POA, replace the scope clause with the single specified act (e.g.,
"to execute the sale deed of property described as ... in favour of ...").
VERIFICATION: drafting structure (not statutory form — adapt to the matter and local
requirements).

2. تمہید: "میں، [نام] ولد [والد کا نام]، شناختی کارڈ نمبر [شناختی کارڈ]، ساکن [پتہ]، بذریعہ
 اس تحریر مقرر کرتا/کرتی ہوں ..."
3. مختار کی شناخت: "جناب [نام] ولد [والد کا نام]، شناختی کارڈ نمبر [شناختی کارڈ]، ساکن
 [پتہ] کو اپنا قانونی مختار مقرر کرتا/کرتی ہوں۔"
