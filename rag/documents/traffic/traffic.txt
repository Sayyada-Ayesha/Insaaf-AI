[SECTION: 1] Motor Vehicle Ordinance, 1965 (Ordinance No. W.P. Ord. VI of 1965) — Regulates motor vehicles and traffic in Pakistan. Source: Pakistan Code / Provincial Excise Departments. Status: CURRENT.

[SECTION: 2] Driving license — Every person driving a motor vehicle on a public road must hold a valid driving license. Types: (a) learner's permit (valid 6 months); (b) regular license; (c) commercial license (for transport vehicles). Minimum age: 18 years for cars, 21 for commercial vehicles.

[SECTION: 3] Vehicle registration — Every motor vehicle must be registered with the provincial Excise and Taxation Department. Registration number plate must be displayed. Transfer of ownership must be registered within 30 days.

[SECTION: 4] Traffic offences — (a) Over-speeding; (b) reckless/dangerous driving; (c) driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs; (d) jumping red light; (e) using mobile phone while driving; (f) driving without license; (g) driving unregistered vehicle. Penalties: fines, license suspension, imprisonment for serious offences.

[SECTION: 5] Road accidents — In case of accident: (a) driver must stop and provide assistance; (b) report to police within 24 hours; (c) FIR registered under PPC for injury/death; (d) compensation claims through Motor Vehicle Insurance or civil suit.

[SECTION: 6] Motor vehicle insurance — Third-party insurance is mandatory for all vehicles. Covers injury/death of third persons and damage to third-party property. Comprehensive insurance also covers own vehicle damage.

[SECTION: 7] Transport regulations — Commercial transport (buses, trucks, taxis, rickshaws) requires: route permit, fitness certificate, and goods carrier permit. Regulated by provincial transport authorities.

[SECTION: 8] Licensing authorities — Provincial Excise and Taxation Departments issue licenses and registration. National Highways and Motorway Police enforce traffic laws on motorways and national highways. City traffic police enforce in urban areas.
