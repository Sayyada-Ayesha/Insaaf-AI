[SECTION: 1] Elections Act, 2017 (Act No. XXXIII of 2017) — Regulates conduct of elections in Pakistan. Source: Election Commission of Pakistan (ecp.gov.pk). Status: CURRENT.

[SECTION: 2] Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) — The ECP is a constitutional body under Article 218 of the Constitution. It consists of the Chief Election Commissioner and four Members (one from each province). ECP conducts elections for: National Assembly, Provincial Assemblies, Senate, and Local Government.

[SECTION: 3] Delimitation of constituencies — ECP delimits constituencies after each census. Constituencies must be contiguous and have approximate equality of population. Reserved seats for women and minorities are allocated proportionally.

[SECTION: 4] Voter registration — Every citizen of Pakistan aged 18+ is entitled to be registered as a voter. Voter lists are updated annually. NADRA provides computerized national identity cards (CNIC) required for voting.

[SECTION: 5] Candidates — Qualifications: citizen of Pakistan, 25+ for National Assembly, 30+ for Senate, sound mind, not convicted of moral turpitude. Disqualifications: convicted of corruption, default on loans/bills, dual nationality (for certain positions), and Section 62/63 of Constitution.

[SECTION: 6] Election schedule — Elections must be held within 60 days after the expiry of the assembly term (if dissolved after full term) or within 90 days (if dissolved prematurely). Polling is held on a non-working day.

[SECTION: 7] Campaign finance — Candidates have spending limits prescribed by ECP. Political parties must submit annual statements of accounts. Foreign funding is prohibited for political parties.

[SECTION: 8] Election offences — Offences include: (a) personation (voting in another's name); (b) bribery; (c) undue influence; (d) false statements; (e) illegal voting. Punishment: imprisonment up to 2 years or fine.

[SECTION: 9] Election petitions — Disputes regarding election results may be filed in the High Court within 45 days of the Gazette notification. Grounds: corrupt practice, illegal practice, improper rejection of nomination, or improper counting.

[SECTION: 10] By-elections — By-elections are held when a seat in the National Assembly or Provincial Assembly becomes vacant due to death, resignation, or disqualification.

[SECTION: 11] Women and minority representation — 60 seats reserved for women in the National Assembly. Provincial Assemblies have proportional reserved seats for women and non-Muslim minorities.
