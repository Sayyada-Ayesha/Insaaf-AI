[SECTION: 1] Intellectual Property Laws of Pakistan — IP rights are administered by the Intellectual Property Organization (IPO-Pakistan). Source: IPO Pakistan (ipo.gov.pk). Status: CURRENT.

[SECTION: 2] Trademarks Ordinance, 2001 — Provides for registration and protection of trademarks. A trademark may be: word, logo, device, symbol, color combination, or 3D shape. Registration valid for 10 years, renewable. Infringement: use of an identical/deceptively similar mark in relation to identical/similar goods.

[SECTION: 3] Patents Ordinance, 2000 — Patents protect inventions that are: new, inventive (non-obvious), and industrially applicable. Patent term: 20 years from filing. Exclusive right to manufacture, sell, import. Compulsory licensing provisions for public health emergencies.

[SECTION: 4] Copyright Ordinance, 1962 — Copyright protects: literary works, dramatic works, musical works, artistic works, films, sound recordings. Term: life of author + 50 years (literary), 50 years from publication (cinematograph/sound recordings). Registration is voluntary but establishes evidence.

[SECTION: 5] Registered designs — Industrial designs may be registered. Term: 10 years, renewable for 5 more years. Novel designs qualify for protection.

[SECTION: 6] Enforcement — IP infringement remedies: (a) civil suit for injunction, damages, account of profits; (b) criminal prosecution (search, seizure, arrest); (c) customs action to seize counterfeit imports. IP Tribunals adjudicate disputes.

[SECTION: 7] IPO-Pakistan — IPO registers trademarks, patents, copyrights and designs. Opposition proceedings: 2 months for trademark opposition. Appeals from IPO decisions lie to the High Court.

[SECTION: 8] Counterfeit goods — Dealing in counterfeit goods is an offence. Customs may detain suspected counterfeit goods. IPO's Enforcement Department conducts raids with police.
