# Azad Jammu and Kashmir — Legal System and Laws
# Coverage: AJK Interim Constitution Act, 1974 framework; AJK codes mirroring Pakistani law;
# AJK courts; relationship with Pakistan. Coverage levels and verification status are stated
# per section. Sources: AJK Interim Constitution Act, 1974; AJK law department compilations.

[SECTION: AJK_READING_NOTE]
AJK has its own constitutional document — the Azad Jammu and Kashmir Interim Constitution Act,
1974 — and its own legislative assembly and courts. Federal Pakistani statutes do NOT
automatically apply in AJK; AJK enacts parallel codes (AJK Penal Code, AJK CrPC, AJK CPC) that
largely mirror the Pakistani codes. Always answer AJK questions from AJK law, not from the
federal statute text.

[SECTION: AJK_CONSTITUTION_1974]
LAW: Azad Jammu and Kashmir Interim Constitution Act, 1974. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
STRUCTURE (summary of the operative framework):
- President of AJK, elected by the AJK Legislative Assembly; Prime Minister and Council of
  Ministers responsible to the Assembly.
- AJK Legislative Assembly enacts laws for AJK subjects; seat allocations and reserved seats
  are as provided in the Act (including provision for representation of Jammu and Kashmir
  refugees settled in Pakistan, as fixed by law).
- AJK Supreme Court and AJK High Court exercise judicial power; appeals from the AJK High Court
  lie to the AJK Supreme Court.
- Azad Jammu and Kashmir Council: chaired by the Prime Minister of Pakistan, with the AJK Prime
  Minister as Vice-Chairman; the Council legislates on subjects in the Council Legislative List
  (larger matters listed in the Interim Constitution) and exercises administrative oversight
  over listed matters.
- Fundamental rights provisions mirror many of the guarantees in the Constitution of Pakistan.
SOURCE: AJK Interim Constitution Act, 1974. VERIFICATION: verified as to framework (structure of
President, Prime Minister, Assembly, courts and Council is well-established; exact seat numbers
and list entries should be checked against the current text).

[SECTION: AJK_CODES_PARALLEL]
LAWS: AJK Penal Code; AJK Code of Criminal Procedure; AJK Code of Civil Procedure.
COVERAGE: SUMMARY.
SUBSTANCE: the AJK codes are modelled on the Pakistan Penal Code 1860, the Code of Criminal
Procedure 1898 and the Code of Civil Procedure 1908 with AJK adaptations; section numbering of
the AJK Penal Code largely follows the PPC (offence definitions and punishments are generally
parallel, but AJK amendments may differ — do not assume a PPC section number is identical in AJK
without checking the AJK code).
SOURCE: AJK law department compilations. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified (parallelism
well-established; individual AJK amendments need the AJK gazette).

LAW: AJK land revenue legislation. COVERAGE: SUMMARY.
SUBSTANCE: AJK revenue administration (patwari, tehsildar, collector hierarchy) and
record-of-rights/mutation procedures follow the West Pakistan Land Revenue Act pattern adapted
for AJK; land settlement and tenancy rules are AJK-specific.

[SECTION: AJK_FAMILY_LAWS]
LAW: AJK family-law legislation. COVERAGE: SUMMARY.
SUBSTANCE: AJK applies its own Muslim family laws administration, with nikah registration,
divorce notification and family courts following the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 pattern
as adopted in AJK (AJK Muslim Family Laws Ordinance — verify exact title); family disputes are
heard by AJK family courts.
SOURCE: AJK family-law compilations. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified (verify exact instrument
titles in the AJK gazette).
