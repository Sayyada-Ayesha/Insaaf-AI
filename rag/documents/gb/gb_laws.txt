# Gilgit-Baltistan — Legal System and Laws
# Coverage: GB constitutional framework (GB Governance Order 2018, successor of the 2009
# Empowerment and Self-Governance Order), GB Assembly and courts, application of Pakistani
# federal laws with adaptations, and GB Council. Coverage level and verification status are
# stated per section.

[SECTION: GB_READING_NOTE]
Gilgit-Baltistan is administered under the GB Governance Order 2018 (replacing the Gilgit-Baltistan
(Empowerment and Self-Governance) Order, 2009). GB has a Legislative Assembly, a Chief Minister,
a Governor, and its own courts (GB Chief Court and GB Supreme Appellate Court). Federal statutes
apply in GB to the extent provided by the GB Orders; GB-specific laws adapt the Pakistani codes.
Do not answer GB questions with Islamabad's exact statutory regime without noting the GB layer.

[SECTION: GB_GOVERNANCE_ORDER_2018]
LAW: Gilgit-Baltistan Governance Order, 2018 (President's Order). COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
STRUCTURE (summary of the operative framework):
- GB Legislative Assembly with reserved seats for women and minorities (seat allocation as fixed
  in the Order) legislates on GB subjects in the GB Legislative List.
- Chief Minister and Cabinet exercise executive authority for GB subjects, headed by a Governor.
- Judicial system: GB Chief Court (High Court-equivalent) and GB Supreme Appellate Court for
  appeals; Supreme Court of Pakistan's jurisdiction over GB is not automatic (constitutional
  status of GB remains distinct; litigation about GB's constitutional status continues).
- Gilgit-Baltistan Council: chaired by the Prime Minister of Pakistan; legislates and oversees
  subjects in the Council Legislative List under the Order.
- Fundamental rights: the Order provides for fundamental rights modelled on the Constitution of
  Pakistan.
SOURCE: Gilgit-Baltistan Governance Order, 2018. VERIFICATION: verified as to framework (2018
Order replaced the 2009 Order; Assembly, Chief Minister, Governor, Chief Court, Supreme Appellate
Court and GB Council structure is well-established).

[SECTION: GB_ORDER_2009_HISTORY]
HISTORY: Gilgit-Baltistan (Empowerment and Self-Governance) Order, 2009 was the predecessor
framework (elected Legislative Assembly replacing the earlier Northern Areas Legislative Council);
it was superseded by the GB Governance Order 2018. The 2009 Order is retained here for amendment
history: original framework -> 2018 Order -> current text.
SOURCE: GB (Empowerment and Self-Governance) Order, 2009; GB Governance Order 2018.
VERIFICATION: verified as to the succession.

[SECTION: GB_CODES_APPLICATION]
LAWS: application of Pakistani codes in GB. COVERAGE: SUMMARY.
SUBSTANCE: the Pakistan Penal Code 1860, Code of Criminal Procedure 1898, Code of Civil Procedure
1908 and related federal statutes apply in GB with adaptations made under the GB Orders and GB
legislation; GB amendments may differ from federal amendments. Do not assume a PPC or CrPC
section applies in GB in the same form without checking the GB adaptation.
SOURCE: GB Orders and adaptations. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: GB_LAND_REVENUE]
LAW: GB land and revenue administration. COVERAGE: SUMMARY.
SUBSTANCE: GB revenue administration follows the settlement/record-of-rights pattern (patwari,
tehsildar hierarchy) with GB-specific rules; land in GB is predominantly state/community land
with special settlement arrangements.
SOURCE: GB revenue rules. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.
