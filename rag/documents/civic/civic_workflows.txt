# Pakistan Civic Services — Citizen Workflows
# Dedicated workflow corpus. Each workflow records WHO (authority), WHERE (office/portal),
# DOCUMENTS, STEPS, FEES (where verified), TIME (where verified), APPEAL/ESCALATION, SOURCE.
# Fees and timelines change by notification — where not verified, the field says
# "NOT VERIFIED — check the authority's current schedule".

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_BIRTH_REGISTRATION]
WORKFLOW: Birth registration (CRC/Form-B basis)
WHO: Union Council / local government office (and NADRA for the Child Registration
Certificate after registration).
WHERE: union council of the child's area; NADRA centres for CRC (Form-B).
DOCUMENTS: birth certificate/hospital record or affidavit of witnesses, parents' CNICs,
parent nikahnama (where required).
STEPS: (1) obtain the birth report/hospital slip; (2) submit at the union council within the
prescribed period with parents' identity; (3) receive the union council birth certificate;
(4) apply to NADRA for the CRC (B-Form) using the certificate.
FEES: union council registration fee NOT VERIFIED (local by-laws); NADRA CRC fee per the
current NADRA schedule.
TIME: union council registration usually days; CRC per NADRA's current processing time.
APPEAL/ESCALATION: for refusal/delay, complaint to the secretary union council / district
administration, then the provincial local government department or ombudsman.
SOURCE: local government registration framework; NADRA CRC procedure.
VERIFICATION: metadata-verified (steps well-established; fees/time vary).

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_DEATH_REGISTRATION]
WORKFLOW: Death registration and death certificate
WHO: Union Council / local government office (death registration); NADRA for cancellation
of CNIC of the deceased and death certificate where issued.
WHERE: union council of the area of death/residence.
DOCUMENTS: medical cause-of-death certificate (hospital) or witnesses' affidavits, CNIC of
the deceased and of the informant, graveyard/burial proof where required.
STEPS: (1) obtain medical certificate or witnesses; (2) register the death at the union
council; (3) obtain the death certificate; (4) notify NADRA for CNIC cancellation and
succession documentation.
FEES: NOT VERIFIED (local by-laws).
APPEAL/ESCALATION: complaint to the district administration / local government department.
SOURCE: local government registration framework. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_MARRIAGE_REGISTRATION]
WORKFLOW: Marriage registration (nikahnama) and NADRA marriage record
WHO: nikah registrar (union council) for the nikahnama; NADRA for updating the family record.
WHERE: union council of the bride's area (registrar's jurisdiction) and NADRA centres.
DOCUMENTS: CNICs of bride, groom and witnesses, and any documents the registrar requires
(guardians' CNICs where applicable).
STEPS: (1) nikah performed; (2) nikahnama registered with the nikah registrar (union
council) with prescribed fee stamps where required; (3) obtain the registered nikahnama
copy; (4) optionally update NADRA family records (CRC of children later uses it).
FEES: nikah registration fee per the local government schedule (NOT VERIFIED here).
APPEAL/ESCALATION: disputes over registration to the union council secretary / local
government department; failure to register is an offence under the Muslim Family Laws
Ordinance, 1961 framework.
SOURCE: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961; union council registration practice.
VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_DOMICILE]
WORKFLOW: Domicile certificate
WHO: Deputy Commissioner / district administration.
WHERE: DC office of the district of residence.
DOCUMENTS: CNIC (and family members' where required), proof of residence (utility bill,
rent agreement, property documents), educational certificates where applying on
education/profession basis, passport-size photographs, affidavit where required.
STEPS: (1) fill the prescribed domicile application; (2) attach proofs; (3) submit at the
DC office (or online where the district portal exists); (4) verification by the local
police/revenue staff; (5) collect the domicile certificate.
FEES: nominal provincial fee (NOT VERIFIED here).
TIME: usually weeks (verification-dependent).
APPEAL/ESCALATION: for refusal/delay, complaint to the Commissioner's office, then the
provincial home/services department; courts can be approached where the refusal is unlawful.
SOURCE: provincial domicile rules. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified.

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_POLICE_COMPLAINT]
WORKFLOW: Police complaint for non-cognizable matters and police misconduct
WHO: SHO (police station) for complaints; complaint cells for misconduct.
WHERE: local police station; district SP/DSP complaint cell; police complaint authorities
(e.g., Police Complaints Authority where established); online portals where available.
DOCUMENTS: written complaint with facts, dates, witnesses, evidence, CNIC copy.
STEPS: (1) submit written complaint at the station (receipt obtained); (2) if not
entertained for a cognizable offence, demand FIR registration (CrPC s.154) and escalate
under s.22-A/22-B; (3) for police misconduct, complaint to the district complaint cell
or provincial complaint authority; (4) judicial remedy (writ, private complaint under
s.200 CrPC) where police refuse.
FEES: none (complaint registration is free).
APPEAL/ESCALATION: SP/DSP complaint cell -> CPO/IG complaint cell -> provincial complaint
authority -> court (s.22-A/22-B application or writ petition).
SOURCE: CrPC 1898 ss.154, 22-A, 22-B, 200; Police Order 2002 complaint provisions.
VERIFICATION: verified as to the legal routes.

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_GOVT_DEPT_COMPLAINT]
WORKFLOW: Complaint against a government department (service delivery failure)
WHO: the department itself, then grievance mechanisms, then the ombudsman.
WHERE: department's complaint cell / Pakistan Citizen's Portal (Prime Minister's
performance delivery portal) / provincial citizens' portals.
DOCUMENTS: written complaint, reference numbers, receipts, evidence of the grievance.
STEPS: (1) complain to the department (keep record); (2) escalate to the department head;
(3) use the Citizen's Portal / provincial portal; (4) file with the Federal Ombudsman
(Wafaqi Mohtasib) or provincial ombudsman for maladministration; (5) writ petition to the
High Court where the action is unlawful.
FEES: none.
APPEAL/ESCALATION: ombudsman -> representation against ombudsman order -> court.
SOURCE: ombudsman legislation (Federal Ombudsman); Citizen's Portal practice.
VERIFICATION: verified as to routes.

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_PUBLIC_INFO]
WORKFLOW: Public information requests
WHO: public bodies under federal/provincial transparency laws (e.g., the federal access
to information framework and provincial RTI acts — Punjab Transparency and Right to
Information Act 2013; Sindh Transparency and Right to Information Act 2016; KP Right to
Information Act 2013; Balochistan equivalent — verify current titles).
WHERE: the public information officer of the relevant body; provincial information
commissions for appeals.
DOCUMENTS: written request describing the information sought.
STEPS: (1) submit the request to the designated officer; (2) the body must respond within
the statutory period (check the applicable act); (3) on refusal/delay, appeal to the
provincial information commission or the designated appellate forum.
FEES: nominal copying fee where applicable.
APPEAL/ESCALATION: information commission -> courts.
SOURCE: federal/provincial right-to-information legislation. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified
(acts exist; exact procedures vary by province).

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_LEGAL_AID]
WORKFLOW: Free legal aid
WHO: Legal aid authorities (provincial legal aid agencies / district legal empowerment
committees), bar association free-legal-aid cells, and courts' appointed counsel for
indigent accused in criminal cases.
WHERE: district legal aid offices, bar associations, courts (application for state
counsel under CrPC where indigent).
DOCUMENTS: proof of identity, proof of income/indigence, case documents.
STEPS: (1) apply to the legal aid authority/committee with case details; (2) eligibility
determined (indigence, nature of case); (3) lawyer assigned; (4) for accused persons
facing serious charges, courts may provide counsel at state expense.
FEES: free for eligible applicants.
APPEAL/ESCALATION: refusal to the head of the legal aid agency, then the courts.
SOURCE: legal aid legislation and court practice (see legal-aid corpus).
VERIFICATION: verified as to framework.

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_EMERGENCY_SERVICES]
WORKFLOW: Emergency services
WHO/WHERE: Rescue 1122 (emergency ambulance/fire/rescue — provincial); police emergency
15 (varies by province); Edhi and other voluntary ambulance services; hospital
emergencies.
STEPS: (1) call the emergency number of the province (1122 for rescue; 15 police where
applicable); (2) give location and nature of emergency; (3) for medical emergencies,
hospital emergency departments treat first.
FEES: Rescue 1122 service is free; private ambulance services charge.
APPEAL/ESCALATION: complaints about emergency response to the district administration
or provincial emergency service.
SOURCE: provincial emergency services practice. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified
(numbers/services can change — advise checking the province's current emergency numbers).

[SECTION: CIVIC_WF_VOTER_COMPLAINT]
WORKFLOW: Voter enrolment and electoral complaints
WHO: Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) for enrolment; ECP district offices for
complaints; courts (election tribunals) for election disputes.
WHERE: ECP district election office / ECP online services.
DOCUMENTS: CNIC, address proof for registration/transfer of vote.
STEPS: (1) apply for voter enrolment/transfer at the ECP office or online (elections
framework); (2) verification; (3) electoral roll entry; (4) complaints on rolls to the
ECP; (5) election disputes to election tribunals under the Elections Act 2017.
FEES: enrolment free.
APPEAL/ESCALATION: ECP -> election tribunals -> Supreme Court.
SOURCE: Elections Act, 2017. VERIFICATION: verified as to framework.
