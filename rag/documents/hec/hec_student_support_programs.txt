The lack of access to high-tech research equipment in Pakistani universities is a significant barrier to scientific progress. Due to the high costs of acquiring and maintaining modern equipment, the Higher Education Commission has launched an innovative program to share scientific instrumentation facilities. This initiative aims to provide the scientific community with access to advanced equipment while also offering a funding source for institutions that make their instruments available.
The objectives of this program are:
 - Provide spectroscopic, analytical, chromatographic and other services to researchers working in different public sector universities on cost plus basis, which will be paid by the Higher Education Commission.
 - Through this program the institutions with instrumentation facilities will get financial resources to optimally utilize their instruments.
 - The PhD scholars in Pakistani HEIs will have access to available facilities within the country, which will promote research collaboration and save foreign exchange, which is otherwise, required.

1. Sign-in / Sign-up
New users must create an account by selecting the “Sign-up" option, while existing users can log-in using their registered credentials at www.eservices.hec.gov.pk. Applicants are then required to select the “Degree Attestation" option from the menu available on the left side of their dashboard.

2. Personal Details
Applicants are required to enter personal information, including name, father's name, date of birth, marital status, gender, and address etc. (Applicants must ensure, prior to submitting the application, that their personal details on the CNIC and all educational documents are identical. Failing this, the attestation process will not be initiated).

3. Degree Details
i. Applicants must provide complete academic information of educational document(s) including level of education, duration, degree title, campus/affiliated college, session and registration/roll/seat/serial number (whichever is applicable) for the attestation of degree / transcript / Provisional Certificate / Equivalence Certificate issued by HEC.
ii. The applicant must select, from the checkbox options, only the degree program for which attestation is required.

4. Attestation Details
Based on the selected degree program, the following document types will be available for attestation under this section:
 i. Degree
 ii. Transcript (Result Card/Result Intimation Card/Detailed Marks Certificate)
 iii. Provisional Certificate (only those issued by the university's examination office)
 iv. HEC Equivalence Letter
The system automatically calculates the fee based on the selected documents. Applicants are advised to select only those degree programs and documents that are required and readily available for attestation.

5. Document Upload
Applicants are required to upload clear and legible scanned copies of their CNIC (both front and back) along with degree(s) / transcript(s) / Provisional Certificate(s) / Equivalence Certificate(s) issued by HEC. Any additional supporting documents may also be uploaded under the “Other Documents" tab.

The attestation fee is Rs. 3,000 per document (degree, transcript, provisional certificate, or HEC equivalence letter). Applicants are advised to carefully select the documents for attestation and ensure that the fee is paid in accordance with their requirements. It is the sole responsibility of the applicant to verify the completeness and accuracy of all attestation details before submitting the application. In case of any incorrect selection, the fee will neither be refunded, adjusted, nor transferred.
To pay the attestation fee, a Consumer Number is automatically generated when the applicant clicks the “Proceed for Payment" button in their account. Applicants should then complete the payment using their preferred online banking channel or platform. After the payment is successfully made, they must click the “Verify Payment" button in their account. Once the payment is verified, the application is automatically submitted to HEC.
Scrutiny of Application by HEC
i. HEC conducts an initial review to ensure:
 - Verification of personal details with NADRA
 - Availability of all required documents
 - Consistency of information across CNIC and academic documents.
 - To ensure the HEC guidelines are followed to issue such degrees.
 - Accuracy of university/campus and degree details.
 - Inclusion of name of degree holder in the PhD Country Directory (for PhD cases)
 - Applicant's name in approved lists, where applicable
ii. If any deficiencies are identified, the application is returned to the applicant with remarks, and a task is assigned in the “Task Assigned to Me" section of the e-services account of applicant. The applicant is required to fulfill the deficiency or upload required document as per HEC remarks and resubmit the application for re-scrutiny/evaluation.
iii. If the application is found complete and accurate, it is forwarded to the concerned university for verification. Applicants are notified via SMS, email, and the system, where they can also track the real-time status of their application.
Verification by the University/HEI
The concerned university verifies the applicant's credentials against its official records and conveys its verification decision to HEC for consideration.
Issuance of e-Attestation Certificate
After receiving verification from the university, HEC conducts a final review. If the case meets all requirements and complies with applicable policies, an e-Attestation Certificate is issued against documents applied for attestation. Applicants are notified via SMS and email for issuance of e-Attestation Certificate. The applicants can download the certificate from their online account at HEC Portal.
General Guidelines:
 - Attestation of photocopies is no longer offered.
 - Documents with overwriting, erasures, or alterations will not be attested. Applicants should obtain corrected/non-altered documents from the awarding university.
 - Transcripts (complete or incomplete) can be attested even if the final degree has not been issued.
 - In cases of incomplete transcripts, the university must clearly indicate the academic status (e.g., degree in progress/incomplete degree program/incomplete transcript). Applicants must also select the appropriate option during online application.
 - Degrees awarded by foreign Universities/DAIs are not attested by HEC. HEC issue equivalence letters against foreign qualifications and ONLY such equivalence letters are attested by HEC through this web portal. The web link for equivalence of foreign degrees is https://eservices.hec.gov.pk.
 - If your degree is in the field of Medical or Nursing, then please upload your professional registration card/certificate of the relevant professional body/council such as PMDC/PNMC, (under heading of other documents) at the time of applying for degrees attestation;
 - For MS/MPhil or equivalent programs launched after 7 November 2013, an HEC-issued NOC is mandatory.
 - For PhD degrees, the applicant's name must appear in the PhD Country Directory (PCD).

Education Testing Council (ETC) was setup by the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan (HEC) vide notification No. 19-54 HEC/HRM/14/584, dated 25th January 2017. Later, the Supreme Court of Pakistan directed the HEC in August 2018 to conduct assessment tests for admission into LLB degree programs and enrollment of law graduates with Pakistan Bar Council. Thus, recognizing the need for a state-of-the-art, independent, financially viable, transparent, and accountable testing service provider, the Commission approved the establishment of a revamped Education Testing Council (ETC) in its 34th meeting held on August 30-31, 2018.
Our Vision:
“Be the enabler for coming generations of students to unlock their true potential in service of the nation, as well as establish and help ensure merit at all levels of education, public service and professional endeavor through uncompromising commitment to quality, equity and fairness in all our efforts.”
Our Mission:
To realize the vision of developing a merit-based induction system for higher education, novel methods involving stochastic modeling and probability theory are essential. These methods will help create testing systems that determine students' merit, aptitude, and inclinations, providing feedback on their strengths and areas needing reinforcement. The system aims to standardize assessments across diverse educational backgrounds and align with international best practices, such as those of SAT, GRE, and GMAT.
Technology plays a crucial role in achieving this goal by reducing human involvement in the testing process. The use of the internet, cloud computing, blockchain, and AI can streamline registration, testing, and result dissemination, ensuring secure, efficient, and timely feedback. An interim plan has been implemented to improve current processes, automating and digitizing some aspects while enhancing printing and binding with better hardware.
ETC Objectives:
 - To develop, Administer and score tests for admission into higher education programs, in such areas and fields as determined by the BOG or as may be directed by the superior judiciary of Pakistan;
 - To develop, Administer and score tests for the assessment of graduates of higher education programs with respect to general skills and specific expertise for the purpose of having a pool of duly benchmarked candidates for employment in public and private sectors, in such areas and fields as determined by the BOG;
 - To provide an objective measurement of students' and graduates' knowledge, skills and aptitude, thereby providing a uniform, transparent and standardized evaluation of candidates' abilities;
 - To provide highly effective and efficient testing services by utilizing latest technologies, at a reasonable cost;
 - To provide advisory services and training/ capacity building on educational testing to relevant stakeholders;
 - To undertake high quality research in the field of educational assessment, including focusing on global best practices, for the purpose of developing more effective and innovative assessment tools; and
 - To develop and evolve into a globally recognized testing and assessment solutions provider.

This scheme is aligned with the United Nation's Standard Development Goals (SDGs) projecting, 'By 2030 eliminate gender disparities in education and ensure equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for the vulnerable persons with disabilities, indigenous people and children in vulnerable situations'. Pakistan has signed a treaty on September 15, 2008 in the United Nations Convention on "Rights of Persons with Disabilities", and ratified on July 5, 2011. As per data of Census 1998, there were 622,025 disabled (crippled) persons in Pakistan. Out of these 21,925 were enrolled in different public and private sector schools, colleges and universities in Pakistan.
OBJECTIVE
The main objective of the scheme is to facilitate special students having ambulatory disability, so that they can be assisted in their movements and contribute effectively towards the progress of the country.
VISION:
Inclusive Development Leaving No One Behind
DESCRIPTION:
Ambulatory disabilities are those that have an impact on one's ambulation. This includes people who limp, who experience pain when walking, who cannot walk unaided, who use wheelchairs-canes-walkers and whose walk is impaired because of a physical difference.
Ambulatory disability means a disability because of which a person:
 - Has a physical and permanent disability to such a degree that the person is unable to move from place to place without the aid of a wheelchair;
 - Is not able to cross curbs because of paralysis or loss of function of the person's legs;
 - Is missing one or both legs; or
 - Has a permanently impaired or unsteady gait that makes it impossible or impractical to walk as a means of transportation.
EXECUTION:
 - The execution and operational phases would be looked after by HEC, via ensuring that the distribution is being made transparently and on merit to those students, who fulfill the eligibility criteria for the program.
 - Based on the criteria, the list of students declared eligible to receive the wheel chair would be shared with the respective universities. The student would also be informed via email and a letter addressed at his residential address to collect the wheel chair through the University's relevant department. The Universities via HEC Regional Centers would be responsible to deliver the wheel chair to the concerned student and forward a receiving to the HEC in this regard.
 - The details of the vendor would be shared with the student, so that any repair or service during the warranty period may be made immediately and free of cost.

Access to Scientific Instrumentation Program
The lack of access to high-tech research equipment in Pakistani universities is a significant barrier to scientific progress. Due to the high costs of acquiring and maintaining modern equipment, the Higher Education Commission has launched an innovative program to share scientific instrumentation facilities. This initiative aims to provide the scientific community with access to advanced equipment while also offering a funding source for institutions that make their instruments available.
The objectives of this program are:
- Provide spectroscopic, analytical, chromatographic and other services to researchers working in different public sector universities on cost plus basis, which will be paid by the Higher Education Commission.
- Through this program the institutions with instrumentation facilities will get financial resources to optimally utilize their instruments.
- The PhD scholars in Pakistani HEIs will have access to available facilities within the country, which will promote research collaboration and save foreign exchange, which is otherwise, required.

FAQs
Q.1 What is ASIP ?
ASIP stands for Access to Scientific Instrumentation Program.
Q.2 What are the objectives of ASIP ?
Under Access to Scientific Instrumentation Program (ASIP), the Higher Education Commission (HEC) supports the researchers to get access to sophisticated equipment. Provide spectroscopic, analytical, chromatographic and other services to PhD researchers enrolled in different public sector universities on cost plus basis, which will be paid by the HEC.
Q.3 Who is eligible to get support under ASIP ?
The facility is exclusively for enrolled PhD students from HEC-recognized public sector local universities. Their synopsis must be approved by a competent university forum (such as the Advanced Studies & Research Board, Directorate of Research, or Ethical Review Board) and they must be admitted according to HEC admission criteria, following the terms and conditions in section C of the policy.
Q.4 Is every student from any university eligible to apply under ASIP?
Only the students of such public sector universities may apply who do not have that facility at their own university.
Q.5 How much funding is available under ASIP?
The maximum funding available for sample analysis for each applicant is Rs. 200,000/year (i.e. from 1st July to 30th June). While multiple applications may be submitted, but the total amount must not exceed the limit of Rs 200,000/year of three disbursements over the course of their program to support the completion of their research.
Q.6 Can a faculty member apply for ASIP?
No. Under ASIP only Ph.D students can apply.
Q.7 What are the prerequisites to apply under ASIP?
- Student's with approved Research synopsis/proposal by the competent forum (Ethical Review Board / Advance Studies & Research Board / Directorate of Research etc.) in university.
- Research supervisor of student should be approved supervisor as per university policy and a full-time faculty member of the university.
Q.8 Any other condition to apply under ASIP?
- Students can apply for such lab tests, for which they do not have analytical facility at their own university.
- Tests could only be performed from labs of public sector universities/public sector R&D organizations.
- If the test equipment is available in the institute and the applicant is not using that facility, he/she must provide detailed justification for using the facility at other institute/test facility.
- The only well-justified proposals duly signed/endorsed by the supervisor are eligible under ASIP funding.
- Student must report results of his/her research samples' analysis, in the research work.
- Declaration by student that he/she has not been funded for desired tests from any other funding agency.
Q.9 Does the applicant need to attach his/her research synopsis /proposal?
- The applicant needs to fill out the APPLICATION FORM for the Access to Scientific Instrumentation Program by providing all required information including supervisor details, research details, sample details, and three preferred sample analysis facilities.
- The applicant must attach one page summary/description of a research proposal with the application form. The summary must reflect the total number of samples to be analyzed during the whole research work.
Q.10 For what types of tests, students can apply under ASIP?
Students can apply for such lab tests for spectroscopic, analytical, chromatographic and other services to PhD researchers enrolled in different public sector universities which are not accessible.
Q.11 From which labs can the students get samples analyzed?
Tests could only be performed from labs, which are either at public sector universities or public sector organizations.
Q.12 Can a student apply for multiple tests in one application?
Yes. Student can apply for multiple tests from one lab by submitting a single application.
Q.13 Can a student apply for multiple tests from multiple labs in one application?
No. Separate application(s) must be submitted if tests are to be performed from more than one lab.
Q.14 For how many research samples' lab tests, can student apply?
PhD students can not get analysis of more than 50 samples for any test.
Q.15 Can a student avail itself of permission for analysis more than once a year?
Yes. Students can apply more than one time in a financial year, but he/she is restricted to an overall limit of Rs.200,000/- in a financial year of three disbursements over the course of their program to support the completion of their research.
Q.16 How much time does it take to get permission for research samples analysis under ASIP?
The applicant must apply at least 12 weeks prior to research samples' analysis.
Q.17 How to apply under ASIP?
- Applicant needs to register/login him/her-self through HEC-Research Grants Management System (RGMS) portal by using link https://research.hec.gov.pk
- He/she will have to complete his/her personal profile by entering the required information.

Q.18 May I chose the university supervisor other than HEC approved supervisor?
Research supervisor of student should be approved supervisor as per university policy and a full-time faculty member of the university.
Q.19 I do not find the lab name from where I like to get research samples analyzed?
The lab you desired, to get samples analyzed from, might not be HEC-registered. You may opt for any other available lab. Your required lab may request HEC to get itself registered (if in the public sector).
Q.20 My application is successfully submitted, what to do next?
Your application has been forwarded to your research supervisor, ask him/her to endorse it.
Q.21 My application is being shown at HEC, what is its status?
Your application is in process. Upon recommendation from competent forum, the decision will be communicated to you.
Q.22 My application has been approved by HEC, but I have not been issued award letter?
Login to RGMS-portal and download your award letter from your dashboard.
Q.23 I have got award letter, how to get research samples analyzed?
As advised in the award letter, present research samples to the lab focal person, get them analyzed.
Q.24 I have received results from research lab, they have asked me to submit duly signed copy of “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form" ?
If you are satisfied with the tests' results, provided by lab. You will have to furnish duly signed copy of “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form", to lab for official use.
Q.25 When should an applicant apply for reimbursement of claim?
Reimbursement is made to labs directly. Applicant will have to furnish duly signed copy of “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form", to lab for official use.
Q. 26 Who will receive the reimbursement ?
Reimbursement is made to labs directly. Reimbursements will be released in favor head of the institution.
Q. 27 How are the payments made ?
Upon receipt of claims from labs, HEC disburses the research samples analysis cost directly to the labs.
Q. 28 How much time does it take, till the lab is paid the charges w.r.t. services it renders ?
On receipt of claims from labs (with proper documents), payment is made by HEC within a month.
Q. 29 How can a service provider (lab) submit claim to HEC ?
Upon delivery of results to applicants and collection of a duly signed copy of “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form" from applicants, lab focal person can submit the claim directly to HEC, within two months time, by providing the following :
- Original Invoice from Service Provider (Duly Signed & stamped by Service Provider/Lab Focal Person)
- Payment Form, Result Acceptance Voucher, Copy of Award Letter (collected by Lab from applicant)
Q. 30 Applicant's funds limit i.e. 200,000 is exhausted. Is he/she eligible for more funds ?
If maximum amount i.e. Rs. 200,000/financial year is exhausted, applicant is not allowed to submit application for further analysis in that financial year.
Q. 31 Student and his/her supervisor are not satisfied with the results, what to do ?
Do not furnish the “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form" to lab.
Q. 32 Where can applicant/service provider find “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form"?
While delivering results to applicant, lab focal person will provide system generated “Result Acceptance Voucher" and “Payment Form" to the applicant.
Q. 33 How is ASIP helpful for scholars/students ?
Costs of acquiring research samples analysis can be a major hurdle for students, if students do not have facility at their own university, they can get access to labs of other HEIs and organizations.
Q. 34 How is ASIP helpful for institution ?
HEI's labs can offer research samples analysis and share state-of-the-art research equipment to students of other HEIs. HEC provides financial support to the universities and R&D organizations for giving access to other HEIs' students, to use their laboratories.
Q. 35 If, student already has an account at eportal.hec.gov.pk, does he/she need to sign up again ?
Students, once registered online, will have to sign in to RGMS portal. He/she will have to go through the process of selection of lab and the tests.
Q. 36 Applicant want to apply again under ASIP does he/she need to register again ?
Applicant can use the same credentials to login/apply on research.hec.gov.pk
Q. 37 I have forgotten my password, what to do ?
Share your CNIC number, name, department and HEI title, via Email to HEC (email ID : asip@hec.gov.pk).
Q. 38 My query is not addressed in the above listed questions, whom should I contact ?
You can write your concern at asip@hec.gov.pk and our team will revert in due course.

Prime Minister's National Innovation Award
I. General Information:
The Prime Minister's National Innovation Award (PMNIA), is an initiative of the Prime Minister's Youth Programme being executed by the Higher Education Commission, Islamabad.
The PMNIA is providing an open opportunity for Pakistani youth within the age limit of 15 to 30 years to propose innovative ideas and get funding as well as technical support to augment their ideas into a business.
Young innovators/entrepreneurs are invited to submit applications for the competition. The objective of the competition denotes new digital, technical, governance, and social ideas that could motivate the youth to use their innovative and novel skills and help society and the country's economy at the national level. This will also help in resolving the local challenges faced by Pakistan through creative ideas.
The objectives of the Innovation Award are;
- Improving Pakistan's Ranking in the Global Innovation Index,
- Foster Entrepreneurial Culture among the Youth
- Transforming Ideas into a Successful Business
A. Innovation Thematic Areas /Disciplinary Domains:
The innovation challenge aims to support solutions to the indigenous problems pertaining to the following thematic areas:
- Food Security (agriculture, nutrition, sustainable food eco-system, etc.)
- Water Management and Sustainability (irrigation, water conservation, waste & water treatment, etc.)
- Sustainable Energy (electric mobility, smart energy, transmission, and grid issues, etc.)
- Urban planning (preservation of urban space and life, planning of second-tier cities & towns, transportation, Waste collection and disposal, water and sanitation, healthcare, law, and order, etc
- Climate Change and Environment (climate mitigation, disaster management, metro-scale pollution reduction, climate modeling, mitigation of large climate change impacts, climate, and disaster surveillance, etc.)
- Information Technology and Telecom (Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Big Data, disparities in excess of technology, 5G networks, healthcare delivery through the use of ICT)
- Innovative Governance and Reforms (responsiveness to citizen services, accountability reforms through Right to Information (RTI), citizen scorecards),
- Sociology and Philosophy (intellectual heritage, material culture, future of work and family, politics, gender, population, work ethics, etc.)
- Health Care and Medical Sciences
- Others
The solution must impact any of the following SDG goals:
- No Poverty
- Zero Hunger
- Good Health and Well-being
- Quality Education
- Gender Equality
- Clean Water and Sanitation
- Affordable and Clean Energy
- Decent Work and Economic Growth
- Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure
- Reduced Inequality
- Sustainable Cities and Communities
- Responsible Consumption and Production
- Climate Action
- Life Below Water
- Life on Land
- Peace and Justice Strong Institutions
- Partnerships to achieve the Goal
B. Award Categories:
Awards are given in two stages:
Stage 1:
- Top 10 winners are awarded the cash amount of Rs. 1.00 Million each
- 40 runner-ups are awarded a cash amount of Rs. 0.5 Million each
Stage 2:
- The 50 winners selected in Stage 1 are inducted into follow-up programs to scale up their ideas and are also encouraged to opt for incubation so that their ideas may lead to startups. The 50 winners may also request supplementary funds up to Rs. 1 million in addition to the award money as per their requirement, to scale up their ideas through Business Incubation Centers as a follow-up program. For this purpose, a tripartite agreement is signed between respective BICs, Awardee, and HEC. The additional funding is given through respective Business Incubation Centers and is tied to startup deliverables and approved cash plan/budget.

III. Proposal Submission and Evaluation Process
The selection of awards is made through an open, rigorous, transparent, competitive, and merit-based process that proceeds through the following phases:
A. Submission of Concept Notes
- Parallel to the Opening of the call, road shows, drives, and interactive sessions are also in progress. The aspiring students learn the core concepts of innovation around the thematic areas of the PMNIA.
- The students submit the concept notes in consultation with their mentors, based on the competition and submission criteria while following the eligibility benchmarks.
B. Evaluation Process
All applications submitted in response to Call for applications are evaluated by respective review panels. The Evaluation process consists of the following sequential steps:
Phase 1 Evaluation:
The evaluation consists of Administrative Scrutiny and Review by the shortlisting committee through Review Panels.
After initial administrative scrutiny, the evaluation committee broadly rate the idea/concept based on 3 key sets of criteria – creativity & innovation; objectives, planning & execution; and effectiveness & results i.e., problem-solving techniques.
Prior to the Pitching Competition approx. 250 shortlisted applicants go through a rigourous Idea Pitching Training.
Phase 2 Pitching Competition:
The shortlisted applicants rationalize their projects during the training before appearing in the region-wise pitching competitions in front of the juries. The partner universities arrange these trainings as well as host the “Pitching Competitions". The project Experts Committee of PMNIA finalize region-wise juries. Approx. 250 applicants prepare pitches and participate in the pitching competition. Logistics support is also provided to the students appearing in the pitching competition.
The young contestants pitch their ideas in front of the PMNIA jury members, entrepreneurs, academic experts, and members from the industry. The Jury rate the idea/concept based on 3 key sets of criteria – creativity & innovation; objectives, planning & execution; and effectiveness & results i.e., problem-solving techniques. The jury members discuss each pitch and recommend the best ones for the award.
After Pitching Competition, 50 top contestants are shortlisted for award.
Awards Ceremony
The top 50 ideas are awarded in a National level Awards Ceremony.
Award Categories:
Awards are given in two stages:
Stage 1:
- Top 10 winners are awarded the cash amount of Rs. one million each
- 40 runners-up are awarded the cash amount of Rs. 0.5 million each
Stage 2:
The 50 winners selected in Stage 1 are inducted into follow-up programs to scale up their ideas and are also encouraged to opt for incubation so that their ideas may lead to startups. The 50 winners may also request supplementary funds up to Rs. 1 Million in addition to the award money as per their requirement, to scale up their ideas through Business Incubation Centers as a follow-up program. For this purpose, a tripartite agreement is signed between respective BICs, Awardee, and HEC. The additional funding is given through respective Business Incubation Centers and is tied to startup deliverables and approved cash plan/budget.
If any of the top awardees opts for incubation, an additional amount of up to Rs. one Million is
awarded to scale up their ideas into reality. The release of the additional amount is based on their approved cash plan approved by HEC.
Incubation of awardees at Business Incubation Centers
The 50 selected winners are further given chance to avail the incubation opportunity in any Business Incubation Center of public sector universities.

Indigenous ELT scholarships
English Language Teaching Reforms (ELTR) Phase-II offered ELT Scholarships of 1 to 2 year duration for in-service English teachers of public sector colleges and universities in the following programs:
- MPhil /MS in Applied Linguistics and related disciplines
- Masters in Applied Linguistics, TEFL, TESL, ELT
- PGD, ADP in TEFL, TESL, ELT
Eligibility Criteria
- Pakistani/AJK Nationals
- Fulltime/ permanent English faculty of public sector colleges and universities
- Teaching English at the graduate and post graduate level
- Maximum 50 years of age, with minimum 16 years of education, with at least one year experience of teaching English as a subject
- Already enrolled candidates into the specified programs may also apply for the scholarships. They will get funding for the remaining period of their study.
- ELTR fellows of Phase-I can apply for higher degree programs (MS/MPhil) in Phase-II only after completion of service period as per their previous deed of agreement.
Who is ineligible?
- The candidates who are above 50 years of age at the time of application or currently enrolled in any other scholarship program offered by HEC/ Government.
- Any candidate who is not from the public sector HEIs.
How to Apply
- Submission of hard copy of application and documents to Ms Rubina Mustafa, Project Manager ELTR Phase-II, HEC. (For Application Form Click Here)
.Documents required with Application:
- Photocopies of all educational testimonials
- Photocopy of CNIC
- The candidates will apply through proper channel.
- For college faculty NOC must be obtained from the Directorate of Colleges and university faculty must submit NOC from VC /Registrar. (Sample NOC)
- Original online bank deposit slip of Rs. 400/- (non-refundable) in favor of the Director General Finance, Higher Education Commission, on Account No. 17427900133401, Habib Bank Ltd, Shalimar Recording Company Branch, H-9, Islamabad, as processing fee.
- Undertaking for the Indigenous Scholarship on the judicial stamp paper. (Sample Undertaking)
- Admission letter of the university offering the specified programs. If the candidate has not secured admission in any university, s/he will be issued a provisional award letter and s/he will be required to submit admission letter within next six months for final award of scholarship otherwise his scholarship will be cancelled.
Terms & Conditions
- The scholarship awardee is not to hold any other scholarship/stipend during the scholarship period of his/her study.
- The awardee has to execute a bond with HEC/Government of Pakistan to serve in his parent institution for at least 2 years after completion of his degree.
- The Higher Education Commission would monitor the Academic progress/ performance of the awardee.
- No extension will be entertained.
- The outstation candidates must take fulltime leave from their respective institutions.
- HEC reserves the right for any change.
- The awardee will be required to submit the following at the end of semester:
- Progress Report (Click Here)
- Semester Results and Final Result at the end of program
Grants & Benefits
- The scholarship covers tuition fee of the participants up to Rs 100,000/- that will be transferred to the university directly after receiving the invoice duly vetted by internal auditor of the university.
- A stipend of Rs 2000/ month at the end of each semester (for on campus months only excluding the semester break).
- In case a candidate drops out or fails to complete the degree, the HEC will get reimbursement from the candidate.
- No TA/DA will be admissible for test/interview.
- No Hostel charges will be paid to the candidates.
