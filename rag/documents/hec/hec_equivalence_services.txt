# HEC
Equivalence Of Pakistani Degrees
Equivalence Of Pakistani Degrees
General requirements for submission of Local Equivalence applications:
Applicant(s) desiring to apply for equivalence must ensure that their educational documents from bachelor's degree up to terminal qualifications are duly attested by the HEC. Afterward, they should proceed to submit an equivalence application at https://eservices.hec.gov.pk/ . The submission process is hassle-free, requiring applicants to provide step-by-step information. The applicant must pay an equivalence fee using the 1-link Payment method, utilizing the consumer ID generated by the system. After payment, it is mandatory to verify the transaction using the "Task Assigned to Me" option available on the main dashboard of eservices.hec.gov.pk. Once this step is completed, the application is officially submitted for scrutiny.
Processing of Local Equivalence Applications: -
Upon submission of the equivalence application, the equivalence team will then evaluate the application as per policy. After approval, a system-generated equivalence letter is issued to the respective applicant(s). The issued letter can be easily downloaded from the main dashboard by clicking on the "approved" button available in the main dashboard of https://eservices.hec.gov.pk . Subsequently, it can also be easily verified on the HEC website via the following link:
https://eservices.hec.gov.pk/#/degree-verification/local-equivalence
It's also imperative to note that attestation of Transcript(s) is mandatory only if the degree itself is not attested by HEC. In such a case, the applicant will be issued a provisional equivalence letter.
Uploading Supplementary Documents:
Applicants may also submit supplementary documents to support their case and upload the back side of their educational documents, where HEC has affixed attested stamps. These additional documents can be attached to the application through the Documents Upload Section using the "Add Another File" button.
Revision Requests sent by the Equivalence Team:
If the applicant (s) has not provided the required documents or if the equivalence team requires further clarification, then a revision request will be sent to the respective applicant. If the applicant fulfills the requirements, the same can be resubmitted to the equivalence team using the "Task Assigned to Me" option available in the main dashboard of eservices.hec.gov.pk .
For FAQs and general queries, please go to https://onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk/

# HEC
Frequent Asked Questions for obtaining Equivalence of Pakistani Degrees
Frequent Asked Questions for obtaining Equivalence of Pakistani Degrees

#### What is the procedure for getting an equivalence letter from HEC?
The applicants would be required to upload HEC Attested Degrees from Bachelor onwards for issuance of equivalence letter along with supporting documents i.e. CNIC, Professional Council Registration, Marriage Certificate, etc., where required. Attestation of Transcript (s) is mandatory only if its degree is not attested from HEC.

#### What is the procedure to get equivalence of a local/Pakistani degree?
Applicants are required to upload HEC Attested Degrees from Bachelor's degree onwards for the issuance of an equivalence letter, along with supporting documents such as Professional Council Registration where necessary. Attestation of Transcript(s) is mandatory only if the degree itself is not attested by HEC:  (https://eservices.hec.gov.pk)

#### Can Regional Centers of HEC issue Equivalence Letter?
No, HEC Regional Centers are not authorized to issue equivalence letters.

#### How can I submit documents for local equivalence?
- Apply through HEC online Portal: https://eservices.hec.gov.pk.
- Deposit Rs.2000/- (non-refundable) on the attached Challan form in HEC online bank account.
- The applicant is required to upload HEC Attested degrees from Bachelor onwards. along with supporting documents i.e. CNIC, Professional Council Registration, Marriage Certificate, etc., where required.
- Attestation of Transcript (s) is mandatory only if its degree is not attested from HEC.

#### What is the processing fee for equivalence of Pakistani Degree/Diploma/Certificate and how to deposit the fee?
Rs. 2000/- (non-refundable) is charged for equivalence of Pakistani degree (PhD/MPhil/MS/Masters/BS (Hons)/Diploma/Certificate) which can be deposited through a 1-link payment method.

#### Can I submit expired CNIC for getting local degree equivalence certificate?
No, the CNIC must be valid.

#### If I have a variation in details on educational documents. Can I get equivalence from HEC?
No, It is mandatory for applicants that Name and Father's Name, date of birth and all other details must be same on all educational documents and CNIC or Passport (in case of foreign national).

#### My University has not yet issued degree to me. Can I get equivalence certificate on the basis of transcript only?
Yes, you can get provisional equivalence certificate on the basis of transcript subject to verification of your transcript from the concerned Controller of Examinations/Authorized officers of respective University/DAI. Final Equivalence Certificate will be issued on provision of degree.

#### Can I get equivalence certificate from HEC for incomplete studies?
No, HEC does not grant equivalence certificate against incomplete studies.

#### I am a married female applicant. Do I need any additional documents for getting equivalence certificate?
Yes, for female married applicants, a copy of Marriage Certificate/NikkahNama/old CNIC before marriage or any other appropriate documentary evidence is essential in order to ascertain her identity.

#### Can I get equivalence certificate from HEC on the basis of transcript awarded through constituent/affiliated college/institute/department of HEC's Chartered Universities/DAI?
In such cases, transcript issued by constituent/affiliated college/institute/department must be verified/attested by concerned Controller of Examinations/authorized officers of respective University/DAI.

#### I have done Associate Degree after 12-year schooling. Is it equivalent to Bachelor (Pass) degree involving 14-year of schooling?
Yes, as per HEC approved policy, 2-year Associate degree (comprising 60-66 credit hours with 04 regular semesters excluding summer semesters) awarded by HEC Chartered Universities/DAIs is equivalent to generally old BA/BSc (14 years schooling). However, equivalence of degrees is determined by HEC on individual basis upon submission of application at eservices.hec.gov.pk.

#### I have done Associate Degree program (ADP) in Education. Is it equivalent to BEd degree?
No, it's not equivalent to BEd.

#### I have done 4-year BS (Hons) degree program after 12-year schooling. Is it equivalent to old Master's Degree involving 16-year of schooling?
Yes, as per approved policy, any 4-year Bachelor (Hons) degree (minimum 124-136 credit hours with 08 regular semesters excluding summer semesters) awarded by HEC chartered Universities/DAI after 12-year schooling is generally recognized by HEC as equivalent to MA/MSc degree in their own field of specialization involving 16-year of schooling. However, equivalence of degrees is determined by HEC on individual basis upon submission of application at eservices.hec.gov.pk

#### I have done Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) Degree. Is it equivalent to old MBA involving 16-year of schooling?
Yes, as per HEC approved policy, 2-year Bachelor of Business Studies degree (comprising 60-66 credit hours with 04 regular semesters excluding summer semesters) awarded by HEC Chartered Universities/DAIs is similar to old MBA (16 years schooling). However, equivalence of degrees is determined by HEC on individual basis upon submission of application at eservices.hec.gov.pk

#### I have done Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering degree. Is it equivalent to Bachelor of Science in Computer Science?
No, both these degrees are not equivalent.

#### I have done 1.5 years BEd (Hons) degree after 16 years non-relevant education. Is it equivalent to MA Education/MEd?
Yes, according to the revised Teacher Education Roadmap, holders of a 1.5-year B.Ed. (Hons.) degree awarded with 54 credit hours after 16 years of non-relevant qualification can be considered equivalent to MA in Education/M.Ed. involving 16-year of schooling.

#### I have done 1.5 years B.Ed (Hons) degree after 16 years non-relevant education. Am I eligible to get admission in MS/MPhil Education?
Yes, as per Teacher Education Roadmap, the holder of BEd (1.5 years) degree obtained after 16 years non-relevant education is eligible to get admission into MS/MPhil Education after fulfilling all other eligibility conditions of the admitting university/DAI.

#### Is MS equivalent to MPhil?
Yes, both these degrees are awarded after completion of minimum 30 credit hours. The level of these degrees is same.

#### I have done 3.5-year MBA after Bachelor /14-year schooling, is it equivalent to MS/MPhil?
Yes, as per roadmap for Business Education, 3.5-year MBA degree (minimum 90-96 credit hours with 07 regular semesters excluding summer semesters) after Bachelor/14-year schooling is generally recognized by HEC as equivalent to MS/MPhil degree involving 18-year of schooling in the relevant field. However, equivalence of degrees is determined by HEC on individual basis upon submission of application at eservices.hec.gov.pk

#### I have done Master of Laws (LLM) from an HEC recognized University/DAI. Is it equivalent to MPhil?
Yes, 2-year LLM degree awarded by HEC Chartered Universities/DAIs after LL.B is generally recognized by HEC as equivalent to MPhil degree in general stream involving 18 years of schooling. However, equivalence of degrees is determined by HEC on individual basis upon submission of application at eservices.hec.gov.pk

#### Is it mandatory for my University to get NOC from HEC to start MS/MPhil/PhD or equivalent academic programs?
HEC only requires NOC for all those postgraduate degree programs i.e. MBA/MS/MPhil/PhD or equivalent which have been launched by Universities/DAIs after November 07, 2013 (List of NOC's is available on HEC website
https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/faculty/Plagiarism/Documents/Updated-list-NOC-Issued.pdf

#### I have done FCPS/MCPS. Can I get admission in PhD?
Yes, the holders of MCPS and FCPS duly recognized by PM&DC (Now PMC) are eligible to get admission into PhD degree program subject to fulfillment of all other eligibility conditions laid down by the admitting University/DAI.

#### I got admission in an affiliated college/institution in MS/MPhil degree program after March 07, 2016. Can I get equivalence certificate against my MS/MPhil degree from HEC?
As per Quality Assurance Division of HEC's letter No. 1-AD/QA/HEC/NOC(Affiliated Colleges)/2016/122 dated 13.04.2016, MS/MPhil/PhD or equivalent degree programs cannot be launched at affiliated colleges/institutes. Any such degree awarded in violation of above instructions cannot be equated by HEC.

#### Does HEC play any role in recruitment process after issuance of equivalence certificate?
No, HEC has no role in recruitment process or determining eligibility for any post.

#### Does HEC play any role in admission of candidates for higher studies after issuance of equivalence certificate?
No, HEC does not play any role in admission of candidates for higher studies.

#### Is HEC issuing equivalence certificate against CA/ICMA and ACCA qualifications on individual basis?
No, as per approved policy, HEC is not issuing equivalence certificate against CA/ICMA and ACCA qualifications on individual basis. A general Notification in this regard is already available on HECwebsite
https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/Equivalence%20Degree%20Issuance/Documents/Equ-Notification.pdf

#### There is an erasing/overwriting on my degree/transcript. Is it acceptable for the purpose of issuing equivalence certificate?
No, erasing/overwriting on any document is not acceptable for issuing equivalence certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions For Recognition of Foreign Qualifications in Pakistan
Frequently Asked Questions For Recognition of Foreign Qualifications in Pakistan

#### How can I apply for recognition of foreign degree/Foreign Equivalence?
Filling of online application form available at https://eservices.hec.gov.pk

#### What documents are required for recognition of Foreign degrees?

#### Where should I post/ send my application along with documents?
System is fully online and there is no need to send hardcopies.

#### What is the fee for foreign degree/diploma equivalence?
Rs. 5000/- for each degree/diploma (non-refundable) and does not include the verification fee charged by some universities at abroad.

#### How can I pay the fee Challan?
1. Pay online via 1 link only.

#### Can I submit my documents to HEC without filling the online application form?
No. It is mandatory to fill the online application form and there is no need to visit office.

#### My graduation information is available online on university's website, is it acceptable?
Yes.

#### I am not qualified for the award and unable to complete the education abroad and wish to continue the same in any Pakistani chartered university. Does HEC recognize incomplete qualification?
No, HEC do not recognize an incomplete qualification, rather one can get "University Recognition Status letter" and submit to the admitting university.
