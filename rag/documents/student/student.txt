Right to Education — Constitutional Guarantee (Article 25-A)
The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of five to sixteen years in such manner as may be determined by law.
Source: Constitution of Pakistan, 1973, Article 25-A (inserted by 18th Amendment, 2010).
Applicability: Federal and all Provincial Governments are bound to implement free and compulsory education up to age 16.
Provincial laws enacted: Punjab Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2014; Sindh Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2013; KPK Right to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2017; Balochistan Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2014.

Higher Education Commission (HEC) — Degree Recognition and Attestation
The Higher Education Commission of Pakistan (HEC) is the statutory body responsible for recognition, accreditation, and degree equivalence of higher education institutions in Pakistan.
Key functions:
- Recognition of universities and degree-awarding institutions in Pakistan.
- Attestation of degrees issued by Pakistani universities.
- Equivalence certificates for degrees earned from foreign universities.
- Quality assurance and accreditation through the National Qualifications Framework (NQF).
- Regulation of PhD programmes and academic standards.
Procedure for Degree Attestation:
1. Obtain transcript and degree certificate from the university.
2. Submit online application on the HEC official portal (hec.gov.pk).
3. Pay the prescribed attestation fee.
4. Submit physical documents to the HEC Regional Centre or HEC Islamabad.
5. HEC verifies with the issuing institution.
6. Attested degree returned/collected.
Source: Higher Education Commission Act, 2002; HEC official website: https://www.hec.gov.pk

[SECTION: UNIVERSITY_DISCIPLINARY_RIGHTS]
Student Rights in University Disciplinary Proceedings
Pakistani students are entitled to due process (fair hearing) before any disciplinary action, including suspension, expulsion, or examination debarment.
Key protections:
- Right to be informed in writing of charges against them.
- Right to respond to charges (show-cause notice must be given).
- Right to appear before the Disciplinary Committee.
- Right to be represented (in some institutions, a nominated representative may assist).
- Right to appeal to the Vice-Chancellor, Syndicate, or relevant appellate body.
Authorities:
- University statutes and regulations govern disciplinary proceedings.
- HEC has issued guidelines on academic integrity and student grievances.
- Aggrieved students may also approach the High Court via writ petition (Article 199) if fundamental rights are violated.
Source: Individual University Statutes; HEC Policy on Academic Integrity; Constitution of Pakistan, Article 10-A (Right to Fair Trial).

Anti-Harassment in Educational Institutions
The Protection against Harassment of Women at the Workplace Act, 2010 applies to educational institutions as workplaces.
HEC Anti-Harassment Policy (2020):
- All HEC-recognized universities must implement an Anti-Harassment Policy.
- Every institution must establish an Inquiry Committee for harassment complaints.
- Students (including male students) experiencing harassment may file complaints.
- The Inquiry Committee must complete the inquiry within 30 days.
- If institution fails to act, complaint can be made to the Federal/Provincial Ombudsperson.
- Sexual harassment includes: unwelcome sexual advances, requests for favours, verbal/physical conduct of a sexual nature.
Penalties: Minor penalties (warning, censure) to major penalties (dismissal, debarment).
Source: Protection against Harassment of Women at the Workplace Act, 2010; HEC Anti-Harassment Policy.

[SECTION: SCHOLARSHIP_RIGHTS]
Student Scholarship Rights and Grievances
Major scholarship programmes in Pakistan:
- HEC Need-Based Scholarships: For deserving students of public sector universities.
- HEC Merit Scholarships: For top-performing undergraduate and postgraduate students.
- Prime Minister's Laptops and Youth Programmes.
- Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship: For students from low-income families.
- Provincial scholarship programmes (Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan).
Rights of scholarship recipients:
- Right to receive scholarship amounts on time as per disbursement schedule.
- Right to appeal against cancellation or disqualification before the relevant committee.
- Right to know the criteria and be assessed fairly.
Complaint mechanism:
- For HEC scholarships: Email the HEC Scholarship Division or visit hec.gov.pk/scholarships.
- For Ehsaas: Contact BISP/Ehsaas help desk.
- For Provincial scholarships: Contact relevant Education Department.
Source: HEC website (hec.gov.pk); Ehsaas Programme portal; respective provincial education departments.

[SECTION: STUDENT_LEGAL_AID]
Legal Resources for Students
Students who cannot afford a lawyer or need legal guidance:
- University Legal Aid Clinics: Several Pakistani law schools (e.g., Punjab University, Quaid-i-Azam University, University of Karachi) operate free legal aid clinics.
- Pakistan Bar Council Free Legal Aid: Available for deserving persons, including students. Contact: Pakistan Bar Council, Islamabad.
- Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman): For complaints against federal government institutions including federally-chartered universities. Helpline: 1055.
- Provincial Ombudsman: For complaints against provincial universities and public schools.
- Human Rights Cell: Punjab Human Rights Department operates a helpline for rights violations.
- Legal literacy workshops: HEC periodically organizes awareness sessions at universities.
Source: Pakistan Bar Council; Wafaqi Mohtasib Secretariat; HEC.

[SECTION: STUDENT_EXAMINATION_RIGHTS]
Student Rights in Examinations
- Students have the right to see their marked examination papers (rechecking/re-evaluation) as per university policy.
- Rechecking fee is prescribed by each university.
- Examination misconduct proceedings must follow due process (show-cause, hearing, right of appeal).
- A student wrongly failed or unfairly marked may file an appeal to the relevant examination board/committee.
- For Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) examinations, rechecking petitions must be filed within 15 days of result declaration.
- For matriculation and intermediate exams, the competent authority is the respective BISE.
- For degree examinations, the University Controller of Examinations/Academic Council is the authority.
Source: Individual University Examination Regulations; BISE regulations; HEC.

[SECTION: STUDENT_FEE_RIGHTS]
Student Fee Rights and Fee Concession Policies
- Right to Fee Concession: Under provincial laws and university statutes, deserving, orphaned, and disabled students are entitled to fee concessions or waivers.
- Punjab University Fee Concession: PU provides fee concessions (half/full tuition fee waiver) to deserving students under specific rules.
- Fee Hikes: University syndicates/governing bodies regulate fee hikes. Unreasonable or arbitrary hikes can be challenged through university representation channels or student unions/societies.
- Refund Policy: HEC National Fee Refund Policy at Higher Education Institutions:
  1. Full (100%) refund of tuition fee if applied up to 7th day of commencement of classes.
  2. Half (50%) refund of tuition fee if applied from 8th to 15th day of commencement of classes.
  3. No (0%) refund of tuition fee if applied after 15th day of commencement of classes.
Source: HEC National Fee Refund Policy; respective university fee concession guidelines.
