[SECTION: HEC_NEED_BASED_SCHOLARSHIP]
Title: HEC Need-Based Merit Scholarship Programme
Authority: Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan
Jurisdiction: All HEC-Recognized Public Sector Universities
Legal Status: HEC Policy Programme
Source: HEC Scholarship Guidelines; https://www.hec.gov.pk

Programme Overview:
- The HEC Need-Based Merit Scholarship programme provides financial assistance to deserving students enrolled in public sector universities.
- Eligibility criteria (as per HEC guidelines):
  - Enrolled in a public sector university recognized by HEC.
  - Family monthly income below the prescribed threshold (as defined by HEC from time to time).
  - Minimum CGPA/academic performance as prescribed by HEC.
  - Not receiving any other scholarship/stipend from another government source.
- Scholarship amount: As prescribed by HEC (varies by programme level — undergraduate, masters, PhD).
- Application procedure:
  1. Check eligibility on the HEC scholarship portal (hec.gov.pk/scholarships).
  2. Submit online application through the HEC portal.
  3. Submit required documents (income certificate, academic transcripts, CNIC/B-form, university enrollment proof).
  4. University verifies and forwards applications to HEC.
  5. HEC reviews and approves scholarships.
  6. Disbursement is made directly to the student's bank account or through the university.

Note: Specific eligibility thresholds, amounts, and deadlines are set by HEC and may change from year to year. Consult the HEC scholarship portal for current information.

[SECTION: HEC_MERIT_SCHOLARSHIP]
Title: HEC Merit Scholarship Programmes
Authority: Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan
Jurisdiction: All HEC-Recognized Universities
Legal Status: HEC Policy Programme
Source: HEC Scholarship Guidelines; https://www.hec.gov.pk

Programme Overview:
- HEC offers merit-based scholarships for high-performing students at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.
- Eligibility is based on academic merit (minimum CGPA/percentage as prescribed by HEC).
- Scholarships may cover tuition fee, stipend, or both, as per the specific programme guidelines.

- Renewal of merit scholarships is subject to maintaining the prescribed minimum CGPA.

[SECTION: UNIVERSITY_WAIVERS_AND_CONCESSIONS]
Title: University Fee Waivers and Concessions
Authority: University Statutes and Regulations (varies by institution)
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Federal & Provincial)
Legal Status: Institutional Regulation
Source: University Statutes and Fee Regulations

General Principles:
- Universities may offer fee waivers and concessions to deserving students based on:
  - Academic merit (merit-based fee waiver for top-performing students).
  - Financial need (need-based concession for students from low-income families).
  - Special categories: orphans, disabled students, children of university employees, minorities.
- The eligibility criteria, percentage of waiver, and application procedure are determined by each university's competent authority (Syndicate/Board).
- Students may apply through the university's financial aid/scholarship office.
- Fee waivers are typically reviewed each semester/year based on the student's academic performance and continued eligibility.

Note: Specific waiver categories, percentages, and procedures vary significantly by university. Consult your university's applicable fee concession regulations.

[SECTION: ZAKAT_AND_CHARITY_SCHOLARSHIPS]
Title: Zakat and Charity-Based Educational Assistance
Authority: Central Zakat Council / Provincial Zakat Councils; Charitable Organizations
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Federal & Provincial)
Legal Status: Statutory and Charitable
Source: Zakat Ordinance, 1980; Charitable Organization Guidelines

Overview:
- Zakat funds are distributed for educational assistance to deserving students through Central and Provincial Zakat Councils.
- Eligibility: Students whose families meet the Zakat eligibility criteria (nisab threshold).
- Assistance may include: tuition fee payment, stipend, book grants, and other educational expenses.
- Application through the local Zakat Committee or the university's financial aid office (which may forward applications to the Zakat Council).
- Various charitable organizations (Edhi Foundation, JDC, Saylani, etc.) also offer educational assistance programmes.

Note: Zakat scholarship availability, eligibility, and amounts vary by council and organization. Consult your local Zakat Committee or the university's financial aid office.

[SECTION: SCHOLARSHIP_GRIEVANCE]
Title: Scholarship Grievance Redressal
Authority: HEC; University Financial Aid Offices; Provincial Education Departments
Jurisdiction: Pakistan (Federal & Provincial)
Legal Status: Regulatory and Institutional
Source: HEC Guidelines; University Regulations

Grievance Mechanism:
- For HEC scholarships: Students may contact the HEC Scholarship Division or file a grievance through the HEC portal.
- For university fee waivers/concessions: Students may approach the university's financial aid office, Dean of Student Affairs, or Vice-Chancellor.
- For provincial scholarships: Students may approach the respective provincial education department.
- For Ehsaas/BISP: Contact the BISP/Ehsaas help desk.
- If the grievance is not resolved internally, students may approach the relevant Ombudsman or the High Court under Article 199.

Note: Specific grievance procedures vary by scholarship programme. Consult the relevant scholarship authority.
