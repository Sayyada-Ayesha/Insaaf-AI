[SECTION: 1] Expanded Directory of Public Legal Institutions, Regulators and Authorities. Sources: Official government websites. Status: VERIFIED (publicly available information).

[SECTION: 2] Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) — Administers federal taxation: Income Tax, Sales Tax, Customs, Federal Excise. Chairman appointed by PM. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: fbr.gov.pk. Annual tax collection target set in federal budget. Appellate forums: Commissioner (Appeals), ATIR, High Court.

[SECTION: 3] Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) — Regulates companies, capital markets, insurance, non-banking finance. Chairman appointed by PM. Commissioners from each province. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: secp.gov.pk. Registers companies, licenses brokers, regulates stock exchanges (PSX).

[SECTION: 4] State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) — Central bank. Governor appointed by President. Monetary policy, banking regulation, currency issuance, foreign exchange management. Headquarters: Karachi. Website: www.sbp.org.pk. Banking Court adjudicates disputes.

[SECTION: 5] Competition Commission of Pakistan (CCP) — Competition regulation. Chairman and members appointed. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: cc.gov.pk. Powers: investigation, search and seizure, penalties up to PKR 75M.

[SECTION: 6] Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) — Conducts elections. CEC and 4 members. Secretariat: Islamabad. Website: ecp.gov.pk. Delimitation, voter lists, election conduct, vote count. Appointments: 5-year term.

[SECTION: 7] Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) — Federal law enforcement. Director General appointed. Jurisdiction: federal offences including: cybercrime (PECA), human trafficking, immigration offences, corruption, organised crime. FIA Cyber Crime Wing Helpline: 1991. FIA Complaint Centre.

[SECTION: 8] National Accountability Bureau (NAB) — Anti-corruption. Chairman appointed by President in consultation with opposition leader. Term: 4 years. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: nab.gov.pk. Regional offices in all provincial capitals.

[SECTION: 9] Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) — Telecom and internet regulation. Chairman and members. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: pta.gov.pk. Issues licenses, regulates consumer tariffs, blocks unlawful content under court orders.

[SECTION: 10] Pakistan Electronic Media Regulatory Authority (PEMRA) — Broadcast and electronic media regulation. Chairman and members. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: pemra.gov.pk. Issues TV/radio licenses, regulates media content, code of conduct.

[SECTION: 11] Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) — Regulates oil, gas, LPG and CNG sectors. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: ogra.org.pk. Licenses OMCs, sets consumer prices, monitors quality and safety.

[SECTION: 12] National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA) — Regulates power sector (generation, transmission, distribution). Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: nepra.org.pk. Determines tariffs, issues generation licenses, monitors performance standards.

[SECTION: 13] Higher Education Commission (HEC) — Regulates universities and higher education. Chairman appointed by PM. Headquarters: Islamabad. Website: hec.gov.pk. Functions: degree attestation, research funding, quality assurance, curriculum development, scholarships.

[SECTION: 14] Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC) — Regulates medical education and practice. Registers doctors and dentists. Issues licences to practice. Headquarters: Islamabad.

[SECTION: 15] Pakistan Bar Council (PBC) — Supreme regulatory body for legal profession. Elected by provincial bar councils. Maintains roll of advocates (Supreme Court), legal education standards, disciplinary jurisdiction over advocates.

[SECTION: 16] Federal Ombudsman (Wafaqi Mohtasib) — Redress of citizens' complaints against federal government agencies. Jurisdiction: all federal agencies except courts, secret service, nuclear agencies. Complaints must be filed within 6 months. Decisions binding unless challenged in High Court. Helpline: 1099.

[SECTION: 17] Provincial Ombudsmen — (a) Punjab Ombudsman; (b) Sindh Ombudsman; (c) KP Ombudsman; (d) Balochistan Ombudsman. Handle complaints against provincial government departments.

[SECTION: 18] Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) — Manages old-age pensions for private sector employees. Mandatory registration for establishments with 5+ employees. Monthly contributions from employer (5%) and employee (1%). Headquarters: Islamabad.

[SECTION: 19] Workers Welfare Fund (WWF) — Fund for welfare of workers. Financed by employer contributions (2% of annual income). Provides: housing, schools, medical facilities for workers.

[SECTION: 20] Pakistan Standards and Quality Control Authority (PSQCA) — Sets national standards for products and services. Tests goods for quality compliance. Certifies halal products. Headquarters: Karachi.

[SECTION: 21] Capital Development Authority (CDA) — Planning and development of Islamabad. Chairman appointed by PM. Functions: city planning, building control, roads, water supply, parks.

[SECTION: 22] Provincial Boards of Revenue — Land revenue administration. Each province has a Board of Revenue. Functions: land records (jamabandi, fard), mutation, stamp duty collection, land revenue assessment, registration of deeds.
