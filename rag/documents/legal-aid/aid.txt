[SECTION: DLAC]
District Legal Advisory Committees (DLACs):
- Established under the Pakistan Bar Council Legal Aid Rules.
- Available in every district of Pakistan.
- Provide free legal representation to deserving litigants.
- Eligibility: Persons who cannot afford legal representation.
- Apply through the local District Bar Association or District Court.
- DLAC assigns a lawyer for civil or criminal cases.
Source: Pakistan Bar Council; Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973.
Source URL: https://pakistanbarcouncil.org/

[SECTION: STATE_COUNSEL]
State-Provided Legal Counsel:
- Under Pakistani law, the State must provide legal counsel at its own expense for accused persons in capital cases (death penalty cases) who cannot afford a lawyer.
- In non-capital cases, the accused may apply for state counsel through the court.
- The trial court assigns a defence counsel from the panel of lawyers maintained for this purpose.
Source: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898, Section 340; Constitution of Pakistan, Article 10.

[SECTION: PAKISTAN_BAR_COUNCIL]
Pakistan Bar Council Free Legal Aid:
- Pakistan Bar Council oversees provincial bar councils and district bar associations.
- Maintains Legal Aid Committees at national, provincial, and district levels.
- Provides free legal assistance to indigent litigants.
- Contact: Pakistan Bar Council, Islamabad.
- Website: https://pakistanbarcouncil.org/
Source: Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973.
Source URL: https://pakistanbarcouncil.org/

[SECTION: LEGAL_AID_ELIGIBILITY]
Legal Aid Eligibility:
- Any person unable to engage a lawyer due to financial constraints.
- Priority categories: women, children, persons with disabilities, bonded labourers, religious minorities, transgender persons.
- No income threshold is formally specified in most cases; the applying person must demonstrate financial need.
- An affidavit declaring inability to pay may be required.
- In criminal cases, the court may appoint counsel suo motu if the accused appears unrepresented.
Source: Pakistan Bar Council Legal Aid Rules; Criminal Procedure Code, Section 340.

[SECTION: OMBUDSMAN]
Ombudsman (Wafaqi Mohtasib):
- Federal Ombudsman investigates complaints of maladministration against federal government agencies.
- Provincial Ombudsman (Mohtasib-e-Ala) investigates complaints against provincial government agencies.
- Free of charge. No lawyer required.
- Complaint can be filed in writing, by email, or online.
- Ombudsman can direct the agency to rectify the issue.
- Federal Ombudsman website: https://mohtasib.gov.pk/
- Appeal lies to the President of Pakistan.
Source: Establishment of the Office of Wafaqi Mohtasib (Ombudsman) Order, 1983.
Source URL: https://mohtasib.gov.pk/

[SECTION: HUMAN_RIGHTS_CELL]
Supreme Court Human Rights Cell:
- The Supreme Court of Pakistan operates a Human Rights Cell.
- Citizens can write letters/complaints directly to the Chief Justice regarding fundamental rights violations.
- The Chief Justice may convert a letter into a suo motu case under Article 184(3).
- No fee or lawyer required.
- Address: Supreme Court of Pakistan, Constitution Avenue, Islamabad.
Source: Supreme Court of Pakistan.
Source URL: https://www.supremecourt.gov.pk
