Verification status: page register read on 2026-10-14; document texts not downloaded.
This register is public-sector employee governance and institutional regulation. The recruitment, transfer, pension and leave instruments are NOT student rules and must not be used to answer a student's admission, fee, refund, harassment or appeal question. The Private Educational Institutions (Promotion and Regulation) Ordinance, 1984 is the only listed instrument that reaches private institutions as institutions.
