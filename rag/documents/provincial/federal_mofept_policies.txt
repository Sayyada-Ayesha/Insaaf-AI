The dates April 2026 and June 2024 for the federal Non-Formal Education Strategy and the FDE roadmaps are as printed by the ministry; they postdate the sector laws commonly cited for higher education and concern non-formal, foundational and TVET education, which is the federal school/skills competency, not HEC university policy.
Education is a provincial subject after the 18th Amendment; these federal instruments do not override a provincial higher-education rule.

Never cite these as binding rules or as a student's basis of claim.
