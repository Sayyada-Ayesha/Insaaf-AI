[SECTION: PROV_PUNJAB_RENTED_PREMISES_2009]
LAW: Punjab Rented Premises Act, 2009 (Act XIII of 2009). COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
APPLICATION: premises in Punjab to which the Act applies, subject to notified exemptions
(commercial and residential premises; certain classes excluded by notification).
KEY PROVISIONS (close paraphrase of the operative text):
- Rent Tribunal: the Act establishes Rent Tribunals; a landlord or tenant applies to the Rent
  Tribunal (not the civil court of first instance) for eviction, rent determination or related
  relief.
- Eviction grounds (Section 17, summarized): the landlord may seek eviction where the tenant —
  (a) is in default of rent for two consecutive months and fails to pay within the period fixed;
  (b) has sublet without written consent; (c) uses the premises for a purpose other than the one
  for which they were let; (d) has committed or is committing material damage; (e) has been guilty
  of nuisance; or where the landlord requires the premises for his own bona fide use or for
  rebuilding that cannot be done without vacation.
- Rent: the Rent Tribunal may fix fair rent on application, considering the prevailing rental
  market, locality and condition of the premises.
- Notice: before filing for eviction on default grounds, the landlord is required to demand the
  arrears in writing (demand notice), giving the tenant the statutory opportunity to pay.
- Time limits: appeals from Rent Tribunal orders lie to the District Court within the statutory
  period; orders are executable as decrees.
SOURCE: Punjab Rented Premises Act, 2009 (provincial code). VERIFICATION: metadata-verified
(section numbers and exact notice periods should be confirmed against the current provincial
code; substance is well-established).

[SECTION: PROV_PUNJAB_WOMEN_VIOLENCE_2016]
LAW: Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act, 2016 (Act XVIII of 2016). COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY PROVISIONS (close paraphrase):
- Protection orders: a court may, on application by an aggrieved woman, issue a protection order,
  residence order, or monetary order against the respondent; breach of a protection order is an
  offence.
- Violence Against Women Centres (VAWCs): one-window facilities for complaint registration,
  medical examination, forensic assistance, legal aid and shelter referral.
- Protection Committee: district-level committees receive complaints and coordinate interim
  protection; Woman Protection Officers assist complainants.
- Offences under the Act are in addition to, and not in derogation of, the Pakistan Penal Code
  remedies (hurt, assault, criminal force, wrongful confinement).
SOURCE: Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act, 2016. VERIFICATION: verified as to
substance (protection orders, VAWC and Woman Protection Officer framework).

[SECTION: PROV_PUNJAB_LAND_REVENUE_1967]
LAW: Punjab Land Revenue Act, 1967 (West Pakistan Land Revenue Act, 1967, as applicable in Punjab).
COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY PROVISIONS (summary of the operative framework):
- Revenue hierarchy: Board of Revenue -> Commissioner -> Collector (Deputy Commissioner) ->
  Assistant Collector -> Tehsildar -> Patwari (field record keeper).
- Record-of-rights and mutations: every transfer, inheritance or other change in land rights must
  be reported for mutation of the record-of-rights; mutation is recorded after verification and
  objection opportunity (inheritance mutations, sale mutations, mortgage entries).
- Fard: a certified extract of the record (fard malkiat) evidences ownership and possession for
  revenue purposes.
- Assessment and recovery: land revenue is assessed and recovered under the Act; arrears are
  recoverable as arrears of land revenue (coercive process).
SOURCE: Punjab Land Revenue Act, 1967. VERIFICATION: verified as to framework.

[SECTION: PROV_SINDH_RENTED_PREMISES_1979]
LAW: Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance, 1979 (as amended). COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY PROVISIONS (close paraphrase):
- Rent Controller: the Ordinance establishes Rent Controllers for areas to which it applies;
  applications for fixation of fair rent and for eviction are made to the Rent Controller.
- Fair rent: determined by the Controller considering the prevailing rental levels, the
  condition and locality of the premises, and the agreed rent.
- Eviction grounds (Section 15, summarized): (a) default in payment of rent; (b) subletting
  without consent; (c) change of use; (d) damage to premises; (e) nuisance; (f) bona fide personal
  requirement of the landlord; (g) rebuilding requiring vacation.
- Payment of rent into court where the landlord refuses to accept: the tenant may deposit rent
  with the Controller to avoid default.
- Deemed ownership protection: tenants of certain old premises have statutory protection against
  arbitrary eviction; decrees require the Controller's route for covered premises.
SOURCE: Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance, 1979. VERIFICATION: metadata-verified (grounds list and
Controller framework well-established; check the current amended text).

[SECTION: PROV_SINDH_DOMESTIC_VIOLENCE_2013]
LAW: Sindh Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act, 2013. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY PROVISIONS (close paraphrase):
- Definition: domestic violence includes physical, sexual, psychological and economic abuse
  within a domestic relationship.
- Protection orders: the court may pass protection, residence and monetary orders; breach is
  punishable.
- Protection committees and protection officers at district level receive complaints and
  coordinate assistance (shelter, medical, legal aid).
SOURCE: Sindh Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act, 2013. VERIFICATION: verified as
to substance.

[SECTION: PROV_SINDH_CHILD_MARRIAGES_2013]
LAW: Sindh Child Marriages Restraint Act, 2013. COVERAGE: PARTIAL.
KEY PROVISIONS (close paraphrase):
- Marriageable age: 18 years for both males and females.
- Punishment: a male contracting a child marriage is liable to rigorous imprisonment up to
  three years and fine; whoever performs, conducts or directs a child marriage is likewise
  liable; a parent or guardian involved in the marriage is liable where proved.
- The Act overrides the colonial Child Marriage Restraint Act, 1929 within Sindh to the extent
  of the higher age requirement.
SOURCE: Sindh Child Marriages Restraint Act, 2013. VERIFICATION: verified as to substance.
