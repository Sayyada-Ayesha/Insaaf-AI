[SECTION: 1] Provincial Laws of Pakistan — Coverage of key provincial legislation across all provinces. Sources: Provincial Codes, Official Gazettes. Status: VARIOUS.

[SECTION: 2] Punjab Laws — (a) Punjab Land Revenue Act 1967: governs land records, mutation, assessment of revenue; (b) Punjab Consumer Protection Act 2005: consumer rights, Consumer Courts, penalties; (c) Punjab Rented Premises Act 2009: landlord-tenant relations, Rent Tribunal; (d) Punjab Local Government Act 2022; (e) Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act 2016: protection orders, shelter; (f) Punjab Agricultural Land Tax Act; (g) Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012.

[SECTION: 3] Sindh Laws — (a) Sindh Consumer Protection Act 2014: consumer rights, Sindh Consumer Court; (b) Sindh Local Government Act 2013; (c) Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance 1979: rent control, eviction; (d) Sindh Domestic Violence Act 2013; (e) Sindh Sales Tax on Services Act 2011; (f) Sindh Culture, Tourism and Antiquities laws.

[SECTION: 4] Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Laws — (a) KP Consumer Protection Act 2013; (b) KP Local Government Act 2013; (c) KP Tenancy Act; (d) KP Domestic Violence Act 2021; (e) KP Child Protection and Welfare Act 2010; (f) KP Police Act 2017; (g) KP Sales Tax on Services Act 2014.

[SECTION: 5] Balochistan Laws — (a) Balochistan Consumer Protection Act; (b) Balochistan Local Government Act; (c) Balochistan Tenancy Act; (d) Balochistan Sales Tax on Services Act; (e) Balochistan Land Revenue Act; (f) Balochistan Local Government Ordinance.

[SECTION: 6] Islamabad Capital Territory — (a) ICT Rent Restriction Ordinance 2001; (b) ICT Local Government Act 2015; (c) Islamabad Land Disposal laws; (d) Capital Development Authority (CDA) Ordinance 1960: regulates planning, development, building control in Islamabad.

[SECTION: 7] Azad Jammu and Kashmir — (a) AJK Constitution 1974; (b) AJK Penal Code; (c) AJK Criminal Procedure Code; (d) AJK Civil Procedure Code; (e) AJK Interim Act, 2018 for governance.

[SECTION: 8] Gilgit-Baltistan — (a) GB Governance Order 2018; (b) GB Legal Framework Order; (c) GB Local Government Act; (d) GB Council established under Article 152 of the Constitution. GB laws largely mirror federal laws with local adaptations.
