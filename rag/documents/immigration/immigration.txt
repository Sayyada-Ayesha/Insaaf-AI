[SECTION: 1] Immigration, Passport and Citizenship Laws of Pakistan. Sources: Ministry of Interior, NADRA, Pakistan Code. Status: CURRENT.

[SECTION: 2] Pakistan Passport Act, 1974 — Passports are issued by the Directorate General of Immigration and Passports. Types: (a) Diplomatic (Red); (b) Official (Blue); (c) Ordinary (Green, 36 pages/100 pages); (d) Hajj; (e) Emergency. Validity: 5-10 years for adults, 5 years for minors.

[SECTION: 3] Citizenship — Citizenship is governed by the Pakistan Citizenship Act 1951. Modes of acquisition: (a) by birth (born in Pakistan); (b) by descent (born abroad to Pakistani parent); (c) by naturalization (residence, marriage); (d) by incorporation of territory. Dual citizenship is permitted with 19+ countries but not all.

[SECTION: 4] Foreigners Act, 1946 — Regulates entry, presence and exit of foreigners. All foreigners must have valid visa/passport. Overstay is penalized. Registration with police required for stays exceeding 30 days in certain categories.

[SECTION: 5] Visa categories — (a) Diplomat visa; (b) Business visa; (c) Tourist visa; (d) Student visa; (e) Work visa; (f) Religious visa; (g) Transit visa. Online visa system available for eligible countries.

[SECTION: 6] NADRA records — National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) maintains: CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card), CRIC (Children), NICOP (overseas Pakistanis), POC (Pakistan Origin Card). CNIC mandatory for voting, property, banking, passports.

[SECTION: 7] Refugee law — Pakistan hosts Afghan refugees under UNHCR arrangements. Proof of Registration (PoR) cards issued. Pakistan is not a signatory to the UN Refugee Convention 1951 but cooperates with UNHCR.

[SECTION: 8] Immigration offences — (a) Illegal entry into Pakistan; (b) overstaying visa; (c) using forged documents; (d) human trafficking; (e) employing illegal foreigners. Penalties: fine, imprisonment, deportation.
