# Insaaf AI — Digital Legal Assistant for Pakistan

Insaaf AI is a web application providing accessible legal information, constitutional rights reference, AI-powered legal guidance, and automated legal document drafting for the citizens of Pakistan.

## Features

- **AI Legal Advisor**: Interactive bilingual advice (Urdu, Roman Urdu, English) with citations from the Pakistan Penal Code (PPC) and Constitution.
- **Pro Drafting Engine**: Automated drafting of legal notices, petitions, rental agreements, complaints, and business letters.
- **My Vault**: Secure cloud storage for saved chats and drafted legal documents via Firebase Firestore.
- **Vakil & NGO Directory**: Searchable advocate and legal aid directory with city and fee tier filters.
- **Constitutional Archive**: Educational guides on fundamental rights, constitutional history, and landmark judgements.

## Local Launch (Windows)

1. **First-Time Setup**: Double-click [`SETUP_INSAF_AI.bat`](SETUP_INSAF_AI.bat)
2. **Everyday Launch**: Double-click [`START_INSAF_AI.bat`](START_INSAF_AI.bat)

The application will start the development server and automatically open `http://localhost:3000` in your default browser.
